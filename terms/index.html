❓ Common Questions

Three questions that come up most often about these terms.

Can I use these guides for a YouTube video or article?

Yes, as long as you link back to the source page and do not read or copy the text verbatim at length. Short quotes with attribution are always fine.

Can I translate a page into another language?

Yes, for personal or community use, with a clear credit and a link to the original. Do not rehost the translated version as a standalone site.

The content disappears with it. There is no official archive. If a page matters to you, save it locally while it is still available.
