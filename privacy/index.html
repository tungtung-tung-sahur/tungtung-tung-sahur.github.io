Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 Effective: Immediately Reading time: 4 minutes

📋 Plain English Summary

  • We do not require an account to browse the site.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information.
  • We only store what is necessary for the site to function.
  • You can request deletion of any data we hold about you.
  • No advertising networks or tracking pixels are used here.

01 Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how this fan site collects, uses, and protects information when you visit. It applies to every page on this domain, including the game pages, guides, character profiles, and community pages.

This is an unofficial fan site dedicated to the Tung Tung Tung Sahur universe. It is not operated by any game studio, publisher, or licensing agency. It exists purely for informational and entertainment purposes.

02 Information We Collect

We keep collection to the absolute minimum. There are only two categories of information that ever leave your device.

🖥️ Automatically Collected Data

Standard browser logs — page views, referring pages, browser type, and approximate region. This is the same information every web server records. It is aggregated and never linked to an individual person.

✉️ Information You Send Us

If you use the contact form, you send your name, email address, subject, and message. This is used only to reply to you. It is never added to a mailing list and never shared with anyone.

We do not collect payment information. We do not run login systems. We do not ask for a phone number, physical address, or any government identifier.

03 How We Use Information

Any information collected is used for exactly three purposes:

  • To display the pages you requested and keep the site running.
  • To reply to messages you send through the contact form.
  • To understand which pages are most useful so we can improve them.

We do not profile visitors. We do not build advertising audiences. We do not sell data to any third party for any reason.

04 Cookies & Local Storage

This site uses a small amount of browser storage to remember your preferences between visits. Nothing stored is personally identifying.

Type Purpose Duration
Essential Remember UI preferences like active filters or scroll position Session only
Analytics Aggregate page view counts to understand traffic patterns Up to 12 months
Embedded content Set by third-party embed players when you interact with a game Varies by platform

You can clear all stored data at any time through your browser settings. The site will continue to work normally — you will simply lose any saved preferences.

05 Third-Party Content

Some pages embed third-party games through an iframe. When you interact with those embeds, the third-party platform may set its own cookies and collect its own data. Those platforms have their own privacy policies, and we do not control what they do.

We do not embed advertising networks, social media trackers, or cross-site pixel systems. If you see an iframe on a page here, it is a game or an interactive tool — not an ad.

06 Children's Privacy

This site is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it immediately.

The content on this site is safe for general audiences. There is no violent imagery, no adult content, and no user-to-user messaging system.

07 Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of any information that is inaccurate.
  • Request deletion of any personal information we hold.
  • Object to processing of your information for any reason.
  • Withdraw consent for any processing you previously agreed to.

To exercise any of these rights, use the contact form and describe what you need. All requests are answered within seven days.

08 Data Retention

Contact form messages are kept for up to 24 months so we can reference past conversations. After that period, they are permanently deleted. Aggregate analytics data is kept indefinitely because it contains no personal identifiers.

You can request earlier deletion at any time through the contact form. Once deleted, the information is gone — no archive, no backup, no recovery.

09 Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in the site or in privacy law. When it is updated, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will change. Material changes will also be announced on the home page.

Continued use of the site after an update means you accept the current version of the policy.

10 Contact Us

If you have any question about this policy, how data is handled, or your rights, use the contact page. The message is read by the same person who maintains the site.
