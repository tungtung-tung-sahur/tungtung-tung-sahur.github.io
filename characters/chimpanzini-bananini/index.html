🍌 Who Is Chimpanzini Bananini?

A green chimpanzee whose body is fused with a large, partially peeled banana. His head and torso emerge from the top of the peel. His arms and legs poke through holes in the banana skin. He chants nonsense in robotic Italian. He is one of the earliest and most recognizable monkey-fruit hybrids in the Italian brainrot genre.

Chimpanzini Bananini — also written as Shimpanzini Bananini or Chimpanzinni Bananini — is an anthropomorphic hybrid of a chimpanzee and a banana. His design is simple and striking: a green chimpanzee head and upper torso emerging from a large yellow banana that has been partially peeled open. In the original image, no limbs are visible. In later animations and fan art, his arms and legs protrude through holes in the banana peel, as if the fruit is a suit he wears.

He is the first character in @alexey_pigeon's lineup of monkey-fruit hybrids. The original video, posted on March 13, 2025, showcased six different hybrids: Chimpanzini Bananini, Chimpanzini Cocosini (coconut), Chimpanzini Ananasini (pineapple), and three others. The video accumulated over 29 million views and 3 million likes in just two weeks. The official playbrainrot.org listing notes that the video has since surpassed 50 million views.

Despite the lineup format, only Chimpanzini Bananini achieved mainstream popularity. The other characters — including Chimpanzini Cocosini, which appeared briefly in a brainrot song under the name Zampolino Cocomelo — remained obscure. Chimpanzini Bananini became the face of the monkey-fruit subgenre and one of the most widely recognized characters in the entire Italian brainrot universe.

🎨 Design Breakdown

Every element of his appearance is deliberately absurd. The green fur on the chimpanzee contrasts sharply with the yellow banana. The peel opens like a jacket. The cartoonish eyes make him look simultaneously confused and mischievous.

🐒

Green Chimpanzee Head

A realistic chimpanzee head with green fur — an unnatural color that makes him instantly recognizable. Large, cartoonish eyes and a simple, often dopey expression add to the surreal humor.

🍌

Banana Peel Body

A large, partially peeled yellow banana serves as his torso. The peel opens at the front like a jacket, revealing the chimpanzee's chest. In later designs, the peel has holes for his arms and legs.

🦵

Limbs (Fan-Added)

The original AI image did not show limbs. Fan artists and game developers added arms and legs that emerge from holes in the banana peel. This became the standard design across all later depictions.

🎤

Gibberish Italian Voice

The original voiceover is a robotic Italian text-to-speech program chanting nonsense phrases. The lyrics are not real Italian — they are gibberish that vaguely resembles the language. The audio became one of the most recognizable sounds in the brainrot trend.

📖 Origin and Lore

The character was created by @alexey_pigeon on March 13, 2025. The original video showcased six monkey-fruit hybrids. The character's backstory was later expanded by fan creators and TikTok users into a full narrative about a forbidden fruit and a cursed transformation.

On March 13, 2025, TikTok user @alexey_pigeon posted a video captioned "Shimpanzinni Bananinni." The video featured a succession of AI-generated chimpanzee-fruit hybrid creatures. First came Chimpanzini Bananini, followed by Chimpanzini Cocosini (coconut) and Chimpanzini Ananasini (pineapple). The video received over 29 million views and 3 million likes within two weeks.

The viral spread was immediate. That same day, TikToker @aimenstaer used the Chimpanzini Bananini audio for their own video, pulling in 1.3 million views and 104,000 likes. Two days later, @neopetlife posted a cuter spin showing baby monkey-fruit hybrids, racking up 1 million plays in short order.

The character's lore was expanded by fans. According to one popular version shared by TikTok user @ai.creature176, Chimpanzini Bananini started as an ordinary chimpanzee who loved to eat. When he came across a forbidden fruit, he ignored the warnings and ate it. When he woke up the next day, he had been cursed with a banana body.

"Chimpanzini Bananini started as a regular chimpanzee who loved to eat. When he came across a forbidden fruit, he ignored the warnings and ate it. When he woke up the next day, he was cursed with a banana body."

Fan-expanded lore, TikTok, 2025

Other versions of the lore describe him as being born from inside a banana peel, or as an inhabitant of the planet Platini Fruitini, where he lived with his siblings: Chimpanzini Cocosini, Chimpanzini Ananasini, and others. The inconsistency of the lore is part of the charm — there is no canonical backstory, only a collection of fan-created narratives that contradict each other.

⚔️ Powers and Abilities

Chimpanzini Bananini is not the strongest fighter in direct combat. But his abilities are uniquely banana-themed. He sheds his peel to reveal a muscular physique, he swings from vines, and he is described in official lore as "highly intelligent and dangerous."

🍌

Shed Banana Peel

Chimpanzini Bananini can shed the bananas on his body to reveal a muscular physique for combat. This transformation significantly increases his strength and speed, but leaves him vulnerable to banana-peel slip hazards afterward.

🌿

High Jump & Swing

As a primate, Chimpanzini Bananini has exceptional agility. He can perform high jumps, swing from vines and branches, and traverse jungle environments with ease. His green fur provides natural camouflage in forest settings.

🧠

Enormous IQ

According to official lore, Chimpanzini Bananini is "highly intelligent and dangerous." Some sources claim he has an "enormous IQ" and can blend into any environment, making him a master of disguise and escape.

🍌

Banana Barrage

In fan-created power scaling videos, Chimpanzini Bananini attacks by throwing explosive bananas at opponents. The bananas detonate on impact, creating a sticky, slippery surface that can immobilize enemies.

🎮 Steal a Brainrot Stats

Chimpanzini Bananini is a Legendary-tier collectible in Steal a Brainrot. He costs $50,000 and generates $300 per second. He is one of the most accessible Legendary-tier characters for players transitioning into the mid-to-late game.

🎮 Game Appearances

From the original TikTok clip to Steal a Brainrot, Toilet Tower Defense, and fan-made games.

🦹

Steal a Brainrot (Roblox)

A Legendary-tier collectible introduced at the game's launch. He appears on the conveyor belt with a spawn rate that places him in the mid-to-late game progression. His design in the game includes arms and legs emerging from the banana peel — an addition not present in the original AI image.

🚽

Toilet Tower Defense (Roblox)

Chimpanzini Bananini is a playable unit in Toilet Tower Defense, a popular Roblox tower defense game. His unit ability involves throwing explosive bananas that deal area damage to enemies. He is one of the most recognizable brainrot units in the game.

🎮

Fortnite Creative

Multiple community-made Fortnite maps use the character. In Fortnite, Chimpanzini Bananini appears in creative maps as a playable skin or a NPC enemy. None are official Epic Games content.

🃏

Trading Cards & 3D Prints

Chimpanzini Bananini has been featured on Italian Brainrot trading cards and is a popular subject for 3D printing models. Multiple 3D print files of the character exist on platforms like CGTrader and 3DGo.

🧬 Character Relationships

Chimpanzini Bananini has a family of monkey-fruit hybrids. Some were introduced in the same original video; others were created by fans or other TikTok users.

🥥

Chimpanzini Cocosini

Chimpanzini Bananini's brother or cousin. A monkey-coconut hybrid who appeared in the same original video. Known as "Zampolino Cocomelo" in some versions. He appeared briefly in a brainrot song but never achieved the same popularity as Bananini.

🍍

Chimpanzini Ananasini

A monkey-pineapple hybrid, also from the original video. One of the six monkey-fruit hybrids showcased in @alexey_pigeon's initial TikTok. He remains obscure compared to Bananini.

🍉

Gorillo Watermellondrillo

A gorilla-watermelon hybrid, sometimes described as Chimpanzini Bananini's brother in fan lore. He is one of the most popular monkey-fruit hybrids after Bananini.

☕

Chimpanzinni Cappucinni

A monkey-coffee hybrid, sometimes described as Chimpanzini Bananini's brother in fan-compiled family trees. His existence is not confirmed in any official source.
