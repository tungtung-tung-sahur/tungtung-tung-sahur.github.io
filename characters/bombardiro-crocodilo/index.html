🐊 Who Is Bombardiro Crocodilo?

A crocodile fused with a World War II bomber. He flies over battlefields, drops bombs, and speaks in rhyming pseudo-Italian. He is one of the earliest and most recognizable characters in the Italian brainrot lineup.

Bombardiro Crocodilo — sometimes written as Bombardino Crocodilo — is an anthropomorphic crocodile whose body is replaced by a twin-engine military bomber from the World War II era. The design is a direct fusion: the head and jaws of a crocodile, the fuselage and wings of a B-17 Flying Fortress, and a bomb visible underneath. He is usually depicted in flight, dropping explosives on unnamed targets below.[reference:0]

Unlike Tung Tung Sahur, who is rooted in Indonesian Ramadan tradition, Bombardiro Crocodilo has no cultural anchor. He is pure absurdism — a creature that exists because someone asked an AI to combine a crocodile with a warplane and gave the result a rhyming Italian name. That lack of meaning is precisely the point of the brainrot genre.[reference:1]

His name is pseudo-Italian. Crocodilo is a slight distortion of the Italian word coccodrillo (crocodile). Bombardiro does not exist in standard Italian — it is a fabricated word that sounds like bombardiere (bomber). The combination produces a name that sounds dramatic and ridiculous at the same time.[reference:2]

🎨 Design Breakdown

Every element of his appearance serves a purpose. The crocodile head makes him recognizable. The bomber body gives him his function. The bomb underneath is his threat.

🐊

Crocodile Head

Long snout, visible teeth, reptilian eyes. The head is the only organic part of the character. It makes him readable as an animal rather than a machine.

✈️

Bomber Fuselage

A World War II-era twin-engine bomber. The exact model varies between depictions, but the B-17 Flying Fortress is the most commonly cited inspiration. The body is metallic, riveted, and industrial.

💣

Bomb Payload

A single bomb is usually visible underneath the fuselage. In the original TikTok clip, this is what he drops. In fan art, the bomb count varies from one to dozens.

🎤

Italian TTS Voice

He speaks in a male Italian text-to-speech voice that rhymes words and narrates absurd, often offensive scenarios. The voice is what made the character go viral.

📖 Origin and Lore

The character was created by a TikTok user in mid-February 2025. The original video was removed in some regions for offensive content. The character survived because the internet does not forget.

The earliest known Bombardiro Crocodilo video was posted on February 20, 2025 by TikTok user @armenjiharhanyan. The video showed an AI-generated image of the crocodile-bomber and was narrated by a male Italian text-to-speech voice that said: "Bombardino Crocodilo, an alligator that bombs kids in Gaza and Palestine and doesn't believe in Allah and loves bombs."[reference:3]

The video amassed over 5 million plays and 543,000 likes within a month. It was controversial from the start — the content was offensive, and the creator later faced backlash. But the character design itself was too distinctive to disappear. Other creators began using the AI-generated image in their own videos, separating the visual from the original audio.[reference:4]

"Bombardino Crocodilo, an alligator that bombs kids in Gaza and Palestine and doesn't believe in Allah and loves bombs."

Original Italian text-to-speech audio, February 2025

Over the following weeks, the character became part of the broader Italian brainrot ecosystem. He was paired with Bombombini Gusini, a goose-fighter jet hybrid described as his brother or rival. Fan creators built out a loose narrative: Bombardiro was the aggressive aerial commander; Bombombini was his counterpart. The two were often depicted in conflict.[reference:5]

By mid-2025, Bombardiro Crocodilo had appeared in thousands of TikTok videos, YouTube compilations, and fan animations. He became one of the most recognizable Italian brainrot characters — second only to Tralalero Tralala in name recognition and arguably more popular in gameplay contexts.

🎮 Steal a Brainrot Stats

Bombardiro Crocodilo is a Mythic-tier collectible in Steal a Brainrot. It costs $500,000 to purchase from the conveyor and generates $2,500 per second — more than double the next tier down.

With a Rainbow mutation (10x multiplier), it earns $25,000 per second. 🎮 Game Appearances

From the original TikTok clip to Fortnite creative maps. Each appearance uses the character differently.

🦹

Steal a Brainrot (Roblox)

One of the most valuable Mythic-tier collectibles. Introduced in mid-2025. Still obtainable through the conveyor or by stealing from other bases. Not removed from the game during the Mementum Lab dispute.

🎮

Fortnite Creative

Multiple community-made Fortnite maps use the character: Bombardiro Crocodilo Pillars, Red vs Blue, and Tralalero vs Bombardiro. None are official Epic Games content, but they show how deeply the character has embedded in gaming culture.

🧬 Character Relationships

Bombardiro Crocodilo exists in a loose narrative web with other Italian brainrot characters. These relationships are fan-created, not canon.

🦢

Bombombini Gusini

A goose fused with a fighter jet. Often described as Bombardiro's brother or rival. Both are aerial characters. Fans depict them in conflict or as a team.

🦈

Tralalero Tralala

The original Italian brainrot character. A three-legged shark in Nike sneakers. Depicted as Bombardiro's arch-nemesis in fan battles, despite the two having no canonical connection.
