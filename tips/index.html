⚡ Quick Tips: One Fix at a Time

Nine fast tips for players who already know the game but are stuck on a specific problem. Each one addresses a single mistake.

🔋

Tap the Detector

Holding the scan button drains the battery in 15 seconds. Tapping gives you 40 separate pulses. There is never a reason to hold the button.

🥖

Bake Before You Explore

Three perfect loaves earn 36 coins. That is enough for a battery and a lucky charm. Bake first, then explore. Do not explore first and bake later.

👀

Watch One Full Loop

The patrol walks the same path in the same order every run. Watch one full cycle from cover before you move. It costs 40 seconds. It saves lives.

🦴

Find the Bone Charm Early

The bone charm reveals relics through walls. It is buried in the Graveyard's northwest quadrant. Find it before the Mansion — otherwise you dig blind.

🗺️

Get the Map Fragment

The map fragment in the Abandoned Village church tells you which wing of the Mansion holds the Golden Statue. Without it, you search all four wings.

🚪

Open Only the Doors You Need

Every door you open makes noise and lets the patrol move faster. Open only the doors on your planned return route. Leave the rest closed.

⛏️

Dig Only When He's Two Wings Away

The 6-second dig is the most dangerous action in the game. Start it only when you can barely hear the patrol's footsteps. Not when you cannot see him — when you cannot hear him.

🔔

Plan the Village Exit Before the Bell

The church bell rings the moment you enter the courtyard. Walk the exit route before you enter. Pick South Alley 80% of the time. It is the safest.

🍀

Buy the Lucky Charm

30 coins for a fourth life on your next run. Worth it if you are still learning the Cursed Mansion. Worthless if you never lose lives in the first three zones.

🧠 The Three Skills That Matter Most

Every tip in this section is a variation of one of three underlying skills. If you master these three, everything else falls into place.

🎧

Listening, Not Looking

The patrol's footsteps are louder than they look. Two distinct sounds — a heavy thud, then a scrape — tell you which direction he is coming from. In the Graveyard and the Mansion, sound is more reliable than sight.

⏱️

Patience Over Speed

The game punishes rushing more than anything else. Every mistake is a decision to move one second too early. The players who finish the game are the ones who wait longer than they want to.

🗺️

Planning Before Acting

Walk the route before you need it. Open the doors before you carry the statue. Find the keys before you dig. The game rewards preparation and punishes improvisation.

💡 Still Stuck?

If you have read the tips above and you are still failing, the problem is almost always one of three things. Check each one in order.

First: Are you tapping the detector, or holding it? If you hold the button even once per run, you are wasting a quarter of your pulses. The detector has 40 taps. Treat it that way.

Second: Are you watching the patrol before you move, or are you guessing? The patrol walks the same path every time. If you are not observing one full cycle per zone, you are guessing. Stop guessing.

Third: Are you digging at the right moment? The 6-second dig is longer than it feels. If you start it while you can still hear the patrol clearly, you will lose the run. Wait until his footsteps are almost inaudible.

If all three of these are correct and you are still failing, the issue is probably the sequence you are following. The correct order is: bake, buy, scan, dig, carry. Any other order loses time. Full details are in the beginner guide.
