Beginner Guide

Everything you need to know for your first ten runs. What to do first, what to ignore, and the three decisions that determine whether you finish the Dark Forest or restart from the truck. No advanced techniques — just the fundamentals done right.

🎯 The One Rule That Matters Most

Bake first. Explore second. Most new players do it in the wrong order — they walk out of the truck, get spotted in a clearing, and lose a life before they have earned a single coin.

Your first action in every run should be to bake bread. Not to explore, not to scan, not to look for relics. Bread. Three ingredients spawn within 30 meters of the truck. Collect them, return to the oven, and bake a loaf. A perfect loaf earns 12 coins. A rushed loaf earns 5. A burnt loaf earns 2.

If you bake three perfect loaves in your first two minutes, you walk into the Dark Forest with 36 coins. That is enough to buy a detector battery (15 coins) and have 21 coins left for a future lucky charm. If you bake three rushed loaves, you only have 15 coins — enough for one battery and nothing else.

The difference between a perfect loaf and a rushed loaf is roughly four seconds of patience. The oven has a visible timer with a green zone. Wait for the green zone. Pull the bread when the timer enters it. Do not pull early. Do not pull late. This is the single most valuable habit you can build in your first hour of play.

"A perfect Ramadan loaf sells for 12 coins. A rushed one sells for 5. Same ingredients. The difference is four extra seconds of patience."

Common advice for new players

🗺️ Where to Go in Your First Three Runs

Do not try to finish the game in your first run. Each of your first three runs has a single learning objective. Complete that objective, then stop.

1

Run 1: Learn the Oven

Do not leave the truck area. Bake three loaves. Watch the timer. Note the green zone. Pull each loaf at exactly the right moment. If you burn one, restart and try again. Your goal is three perfect loaves in a row. You do not need to explore yet.

2

Run 2: Learn the Patrol

Bake two loaves. Then walk to the northern watchtower in the Dark Forest. Climb it. Watch the patrol complete one full loop — about 40 seconds. Do not move while watching. Do not try to collect anything. Just watch. Then return to the truck and end the run.

3

Run 3: Learn the Detector

Bake three loaves. Buy a battery with the 15 coins. Now sweep the Dark Forest's east side in a grid. Tap the detector every three steps. Do not dig anything. You are learning how the arrow moves. When you find a signal, note where it was and how many pulses it took to find. Do not worry about the relic itself.

4

Run 4: First Real Attempt

Now you have the fundamentals. Bake three loaves, buy a battery, watch the patrol, and try to reach the Graveyard. This is your first attempt at actually progressing. Expect to fail. Failure in run 4 is normal — the goal is to see the Graveyard, not to survive it.

🥖 Bread Baking: The Timing Window

The oven has a visible timer with a green zone. The green zone is the window for a perfect loaf. It lasts approximately four seconds. Here is how to hit it every time.

⏱️

The Green Zone

A green stripe appears on the timer roughly two-thirds of the way through the baking process. It is clearly visible and easy to spot. When the needle enters the green zone, press the pull button. That is the entire mechanic.

🎯

Aim for the Middle

Do not pull at the start of the green zone. Do not pull at the end. Aim for the middle. The middle of the window is the safest point — if your reaction time is slightly slow or fast, you still land in the perfect range.

🔥

Burnt vs Rushed

Pulling early gives you a rushed loaf (5 coins). Pulling late gives you a burnt loaf (2 coins). The difference matters: three rushed loaves earn 15 coins, but three perfect loaves earn 36. Over ten runs, that difference compounds into thousands.

🍞

Bake One at a Time

You do not need to collect all three ingredients before baking. Bake one loaf, sell it, collect the next ingredient, bake again. This spreads your exposure across the run and keeps you closer to the truck if the patrol catches you.

🔋 The Detector: What Not to Do

The metal detector has 40 pulses per run. Most new players waste 25 of them in the first five minutes. Here are the three habits to break before they form.

🚫

Do Not Hold the Button

Holding the scan drains the battery in 15 seconds. Tapping it gives you 40 pulses. Always tap. Always. There is never a reason to hold the scan button, even when you are standing still.

🚫

Do Not Scan the Dark Forest

The Dark Forest has three coin caches. They are not worth the pulses. Skip scanning here entirely. Save your 40 pulses for the Graveyard and the Cursed Mansion, where the valuable relics actually are.

🚫

Do Not Dig Every Signal

If you scan and the arrow points to a relic, you do not have to dig it. Not every relic is worth the time. Coin caches in the Graveyard are worth 15 to 25 coins. If you already have 40 coins, skip them.

💰 What to Buy With Your Coins

The truck shop sells three items. Here is the order you should buy them in your first ten runs.

Priority Item Cost When to Buy
1st 🔋 Detector Battery 15 coins Before your first Graveyard run
2nd 🍀 Lucky Charm 30 coins Once you have reached the Cursed Mansion at least once
3rd 🔧 Dig Speed Upgrade 50 coins After you have beaten the game once

Do not buy cosmetics. Do not buy anything labeled "decorative" or "collector's edition". Your coins should go to items that affect gameplay. Everything else is a waste in your first ten runs.

🚫 Five Mistakes That New Players Make

These are the five most common mistakes in the first ten runs. Every one of them is fixable in under a minute once you know about it.

1

Sprinting Away From Danger

The instinct is to run when you see the patrol. This is wrong. Running makes you more visible. Crouch behind the nearest cover and stay still. The patrol reacts to movement, not sound. Stillness is safety.

2

Exploring Before Baking

Players who explore first run out of coins before they reach the Graveyard. Bake first. Always bake first. The truck is the safest place on the map, and the oven does not move.

3

Ignoring the Watchtower

The northern watchtower in the Dark Forest gives you a full view of the patrol loop. Climbing it costs about 8 seconds. It saves you 40 seconds of guessing. Use it every run.

4

Hoarding Coins

Coins do nothing while they sit in your inventory. Spend them on batteries before you enter the Graveyard. The Graveyard has the bone charm, and the bone charm needs detector pulses to find.

5

Quitting After One Loss

You will lose lives. You will lose runs. The first three zones are designed to teach you through failure. If you lose a life in the Dark Forest, that is not a sign the game is too hard — it is a sign you moved too early.

6

Skipping the Tutorial Zone

The Dark Forest is the only zone where you can make mistakes and still recover. Skipping it by rushing to the Graveyard means you learn the harder mechanics in a harder environment. Take your time in the Forest.
