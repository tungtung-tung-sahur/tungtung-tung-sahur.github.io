🏆 The Three Endings

The game has three endings. You get one of them at the end of every run — the game decides which one based on what you did during the run. Most players only ever see two of them.

1

Golden Ending

The standard ending. You dig up the Golden Statue and carry it back to the truck. This is the ending most players get on their first successful run. It plays a short cutscene: the statue is placed in the truck, the screen fades to gold, and the run ends. Nothing extra happens.

2

Bad Ending

You lose all three lives before reaching the truck. The screen fades to red, and the kentongan's silhouette appears on the horizon. This ending is not a failure state — it is a canonical ending. The game tracks how many times you have seen it and shows a different message each time.

3

Secret Ending

The third ending. It requires a specific sequence of actions that the game never tells you about. The ending itself is short — about 15 seconds — but it changes how you understand the story. Details are below.

To unlock the Secret Ending, you must complete a run without ever losing a life. You must also find the bone charm in the Graveyard, retrieve the map fragment from the Abandoned Village, and dig up the Golden Statue without ever being spotted by the kentongan. If you are spotted even once — even if you escape — the secret ending is locked for that run.

The secret ending itself is a single shot: the baker places the Golden Statue on the truck, but instead of driving away, he turns and looks back at the forest. The kentongan is standing at the edge of the treeline. Neither character moves. The screen fades to black. That is the entire ending.

"The statue weighs nothing. But you will feel every step back to the truck."

In-game hint shown when the statue is first lifted

💎 Hidden Relics the Detector Cannot Find

Four relics do not appear on the metal detector. They are hidden in fixed locations and require manual searching. Each one unlocks something different.

📜

Baker's Note — Dark Forest

Hidden inside the north watchtower, under a loose floorboard. You cannot see it. You must walk to the exact spot on the upper platform and press the interact button. The note contains a single sentence about the baker's missing brother. It does not affect gameplay — it is pure lore.

🕯️

Grave Candle — Graveyard

On the southernmost grave in the west row. A small unlit candle. You can pick it up and light it. The candle burns for the rest of the run and slightly increases your visibility in fog — about 3 extra meters. Most players never find it because it does not glow on the detector.

🎵

Music Box — Abandoned Village

Inside the church, behind the altar. A small wooden box that plays a lullaby when opened. The music attracts the kentongan from 20 meters away. It is a trap, not a tool. But picking it up unlocks a music track in the game's audio menu.

🖼️

Portrait Fragment — Cursed Mansion

In the West Wing ballroom, behind a torn painting. A small square of canvas showing half of a face. If you carry it to the Graveyard in the next run, the graves rearrange themselves into a pattern that points toward the Golden Statue's wing. This is the most useful hidden relic in the game.

🥚 Easter Eggs Across All Four Zones

Six small details that reference the wider brainrot universe or the game's own development. None of them affect gameplay. All of them are hidden in plain sight.

🦈

Tralalero Tralala Silhouette

In the Dark Forest, near the north watchtower, a faint shark-shaped shadow appears on a tree trunk if you stand in a specific spot at the right angle. It is a reference to Tralalero Tralala, the first Italian brainrot character.

☕

Ballerina Cappuccina Mural

In the Abandoned Village, on the side of the easternmost house, there is a faded mural of a ballerina holding a coffee cup. It is partially covered by vines. You can see it clearly only when standing directly in front of the house.

🥥

Capybarello Cocosini Carving

In the Graveyard, on the back of the largest mausoleum, there is a small carving of a capybara inside a coconut. It is the size of a thumb and easy to miss. The carving is identical to the official Capybarello Cocosini design.

🐊

Bombardiro Crocodilo Poster

In the Cursed Mansion, in the East Wing hallway, a torn recruitment poster shows a crocodile with airplane wings. The text is illegible. The design matches Bombardiro Crocodilo, the crocodile-bomber hybrid.

🍌

Chimpanzini Bananini Drawing

In the Abandoned Village church, on a pew near the back, a child's drawing shows a green monkey inside a banana peel. This is Chimpanzini Bananini, the banana chimp character.

🪵

The Nine Tungs

In any zone, if you stand still for exactly 60 seconds without moving, the ambient audio slowly shifts into a low rhythmic drumming. Nine distinct drumbeats. Then it stops. This is the only place in the game where the full nine-tung rhythm plays.

🎬 The Perfect Run Bonus Animation

There is one bonus animation in the game. It plays only if you complete a run with a perfect score — no lives lost, no detections, all relics found, and the statue returned to the truck in under 12 minutes.

The animation is short. About 8 seconds. The baker sits on the back of the truck, statue beside him, watching the sun rise over the Dark Forest. The kentongan appears in the distance — not as a threat, but as a silhouette against the dawn. Neither character moves. The animation fades to black.

It does not unlock anything. It does not give you a reward. It is a moment of rest — the only one in the entire game. If you play enough runs to earn it, you already know why it matters.

To check your progress toward the perfect run, the game tracks five statistics in the pause menu: lives lost, times detected, relics found, statue found, and total run time. All five must be at their maximum or minimum for the bonus to trigger.

🔍 How to Find the Hidden Relics

The four hidden relics do not appear on the metal detector. Here is exactly where each one is and what it does.

1

Baker's Note

Location: Dark Forest, north watchtower, upper platform. Walk to the northeast corner of the platform and press interact. The note is under a loose board. Effect: lore only, no gameplay change.

2

Grave Candle

Location: Graveyard, westernmost grave in the south row. Look for a small unlit candle on the gravestone. Pick it up and light it. Effect: +3 meters visibility in fog for the rest of the run.

3

Music Box

Location: Abandoned Village, church, behind the altar. Interact with the small wooden box. Effect: attracts the kentongan from 20 meters away. Unlocks a music track in the audio menu. This is a trap — do not pick it up on a serious run.

4

Portrait Fragment

Location: Cursed Mansion, West Wing ballroom, behind the torn painting on the north wall. Interact with the painting to reveal the fragment. Effect: if carried to the Graveyard in a later run, the graves rearrange into a pattern pointing to the Golden Statue's wing. The most useful hidden relic.
