Avoid the Kentongan

The wooden character cannot hear you. He only sees movement. That single fact is the entire stealth system — and most players still get caught because they do not know how to use it. This guide covers audio cues, cover types, zone-specific patrol routes, and what to do if you are spotted.

🧠 How Detection Actually Works

Three separate systems decide whether the kentongan finds you: movement, sound, and line of sight. Understanding which one is triggering him is the difference between surviving a close call and losing a life.

👣

Movement Detection

The primary system. Any movement within 15 meters in his direct line of sight triggers detection. Crouching does not reduce this range — it only prevents the noise of footsteps. If he can see you, crouching does not save you. You must break line of sight.

🔊

Sound Detection

The secondary system. Doors, digging, and sprinting produce sound. He investigates sounds within 12 meters — or up to 25 meters for loud sounds like forcing a locked door. Sound does not trigger immediate detection, but it pulls him toward your location.

👁️

Line of Sight

Direct line of sight overrides everything. If you are standing in an open clearing and he turns toward you, you are seen — even if you are crouched and silent. Cover is not optional. It is the only defense against visual detection.

🎯

Priority Order

The game checks line of sight first, then movement, then sound. That means the most dangerous situation is being seen. The safest situation is being out of sight and still. Crouching behind a tree beats crouching in the open every time.

👂 Audio Cues: How to Hear Him Before You See Him

The kentongan has a distinct footstep rhythm. Two sounds, repeating: a heavy thud followed by a light scrape. Once you learn the pattern, you can track his position through walls, fog, and corridors without ever looking at him.

🦶

The Thud-Scrape Rhythm

Each step produces two sounds: a heavy thud when his foot lands, and a light scrape as it drags forward. The rhythm is slow and steady — roughly one step per second. Faster than that means he is chasing. Slower means he is turning or pausing.

📢

Volume Tells Distance

Footsteps are audible from about 20 meters. At 15 meters, they are clear. At 10 meters, they are loud. At 5 meters, you should already be in cover. Use volume as your distance gauge — not sight.

🌀

Direction Tells Position

Wear headphones if possible. The stereo mix places his footsteps slightly left or right depending on which way he is walking. If you hear him in your left ear, he is on your left. Move the opposite direction.

🔔

Special Sounds

Three non-footstep sounds matter: the church bell in the Abandoned Village (pulls him toward the church), doors being opened (yours or his — both are audible), and the dig scrape in the Cursed Mansion. Each one changes his route.

🛖 Cover Types: What Actually Blocks Detection

Not all cover is equal. Some objects block line of sight completely. Others only partially. Knowing which is which is the difference between a safe move and a wasted life.

1

Full Cover — Trees

Thick tree trunks block line of sight entirely. Standing behind one makes you invisible to the kentongan — even if he is 5 meters away. This is the safest cover type in the game. Use it whenever possible.

2

Full Cover — Hiding Huts

Wooden huts in the Dark Forest and mausoleums in the Graveyard are complete cover. You can enter them, and he cannot. This is the only cover type that is 100% safe, even if he is standing directly outside.

3

Partial Cover — Low Walls

Low walls, crates, and gravestones block line of sight only if you are crouched. Standing behind them exposes your head and shoulders. Always crouch behind partial cover. Never stand.

4

False Cover — Tall Grass

Tall grass does not block line of sight. It only hides your silhouette partially. If he is within 10 meters, he will still see you through the grass. Do not trust it. Use it only for brief pauses, never for a full patrol cycle.

🗺️ Zone-Specific Patrol Routes

Each zone has a unique patrol loop. The routes below describe his exact path in order, so you can plan your movement before you take a single step.

🌲

Dark Forest — North to South

He starts at the north edge, walks south along the main dirt path, crosses the central clearing, follows the eastern tree line north, and pauses at the top for 3 seconds. Full loop: 40 seconds. Safe windows: 12 seconds on the west side during phases 2–3.

🪦

Graveyard — Five Tight Phases

He enters through the north gate, circles the central tomb, follows the east row north, crosses to the west row, walks south, and exits through the north gate. Full loop: 25 seconds. The west row is the safest place to move because it has the fewest hiding spots and he spends the least time there.

🏚️

Abandoned Village — Before and After Bell

Before the bell rings, he walks a standard village loop with three phases. After the bell rings, he stops the loop and walks directly toward the church from the north. Once you exit the courtyard, he returns to his normal loop after a 45-second cooldown.

🏰

Cursed Mansion — Wing to Wing

He patrols each wing in sequence: North, East, South, West. He spends roughly 15 seconds in each. If you are in the same wing as him, you have about 10 seconds before he turns a corner and sees you. Move between wings only when he is in the opposite pair.

🚨 What to Do When You Are Spotted

The worst possible reaction is to sprint. Sprinting makes you more visible and drains your stamina. Instead, follow this three-step sequence.

1

Step 1: Break Line of Sight

Immediately move to the nearest full cover — a tree, a hut, or a mausoleum. Do not try to outrun him. Do not look back. Your only goal is to get something solid between you and him within 2 seconds.

2

Step 2: Stay Still and Crouch

Once you are behind cover, crouch and stop moving entirely. He will walk toward your last known position and pause there for 3 to 5 seconds. Do not move during this window. Any movement resets his detection state.

3

Step 3: Wait for the Turn

After 5 seconds at your last known position, he turns away and resumes his patrol loop. Wait one more second after he turns to make sure he is actually leaving. Then move in the opposite direction of his walking path.

4

If You Are in the Open

If there is no cover nearby and he has already seen you, crouch and walk slowly toward the nearest cover. Do not sprint. Walking reduces your silhouette and gives you a small chance that he loses track of you if he looks away for even a moment.

🚫 Three Behaviors That Guarantee Detection

These three actions have no counterplay. Each one puts you directly into his detection range with no way out except losing a life.

🏃

Sprinting in the Open

Sprinting does not make you faster than him. It only makes you more visible. If he is looking in your direction, sprinting is the same as waving. Never sprint unless you are already out of sight and need to cross a 5-meter gap.

🚪

Opening Doors He Is Near

Opening a door produces sound at 12 meters. If he is within that range, he walks toward you immediately. Before opening any door, listen for footsteps. If you can hear him, do not open the door — wait.

🌫️

Moving Through Fog

The Graveyard fog hides him from you but not you from him. His visual detection range in fog stays the same — 15 meters. If you walk through fog without a plan, you are gambling. Move 3 meters at a time and listen after each step.

🎧 Headphone Tips

The game is fully playable with speakers, but headphones give you a meaningful advantage. Here is what to listen for.

Left and right channel separation matters. If you wear headphones, his footsteps will be slightly louder in one ear than the other. That channel tells you which side of the map he is on. Speakers do not give you this information as clearly.

The scrape sound is more directional than the thud. The heavy thud spreads across both channels equally. The light scrape is sharper and directional. Focus on the scrape to determine his exact direction.

Ambient sound masks his footsteps. The Graveyard has a wind loop. The Cursed Mansion has a low hum. These ambient sounds raise the volume floor, which means you need to listen more carefully during those zones. Turn the ambient audio slider down in settings if you are struggling to hear footsteps.

"He cannot hear you. But you can hear him. That is the only advantage you get. Use it."
