Detector Battery

In Tung Tung Sahur, the metal detector is your only way to find buried relics — and it runs on a strict 40-pulse budget for the entire run. A detector battery adds 15 pulses back. Knowing when to buy one, when to find one, and when to save them for the Golden Statue is the difference between a completed run and a lost one in the Cursed Mansion.

🔋 The 40-Pulse Budget in Tung Tung Sahur

Forty cells. Every tap of the scan button is one cell. Every cell is shared across the Dark Forest, Graveyard, Abandoned Village, and Cursed Mansion — spending them early means searching blind when the wooden kentongan is closing in.

Tung Tung Sahur Detector Budget · Per Run

40 Pulses · Shared Across All Zones
Spent in Forest & Graveyard Saved for the Cursed Mansion

Every pulse you tap in Tung Tung Sahur is gone for the rest of the run. There is no passive recharge, no way to regenerate them, and only one item — the battery — that gives any of them back. The Golden Statue only appears on the detector in the final three minutes of a run. That means every pulse you burn in the Dark Forest is a pulse you cannot use when the wooden kentongan is patrolling the Mansion corridors.

Most new players empty their detector in the first five minutes. They sweep the Dark Forest top to bottom, find three coin caches, and enter the Graveyard with 20 pulses left. By the time they reach the Cursed Mansion, they have zero. The result is a blind dig in a corridor while the patrol closes in from both sides — and a lost run that felt unlucky but was actually just bad pulse management.

The fix is not complicated. It is just discipline. Scan less in the Forest. Spend everything you have in the Graveyard looking for the bone charm. Save at least 10 for the Mansion. That is the entire pulse strategy for Tung Tung Sahur.

📍 Where Batteries Spawn in Tung Tung Sahur

Three zones hold a battery recharge. Each one appears in a fixed location. None require the metal detector to find — they are surface items, and they glow faintly when you get close.

🛖
Zone 1

West Cabin

Dark Forest. A battery recharge sits inside the western cabin, roughly 25 meters from the truck. Often the first one a new player finds in Tung Tung Sahur.

🪦
Zone 2

East Well

Graveyard. A battery recharge sits beside the eastern well. Worth grabbing only if you have already found the bone charm — otherwise save your pulses for the charm.

🏚️
Zone 3

Village Well

Abandoned Village. A battery recharge sits near the well on the eastern side. Optional pickup — most runs do not need a third battery.

Each battery restores 15 pulses — more than a third of your original budget. If you collect all three across a run, your total pulse pool becomes 85. That is enough to sweep every zone in Tung Tung Sahur twice over and still have room for the final statue dig in the Cursed Mansion.

The trade-off: each battery pickup costs time. Walking to the West Cabin takes about 15 seconds. The East Well is roughly 20 seconds from the Graveyard entrance. The Village Well is roughly 25 seconds. You are spending run time to save detector pulses — and while you are away from cover, the wooden kentongan is still walking his loop.

🔥 What Burns Your Pulses

Three habits that account for most wasted pulses in Tung Tung Sahur runs. All three are easy to break once you notice them.

Costs ~40 pulses 🚫

Holding the Scan Button

Holding the scan drains the entire battery in about 15 seconds. One long press = your whole run's worth of pulses. There is never a reason to hold it. Always tap.

Costs ~10 pulses 🔁

Scanning Ground You Already Scanned

The detector has a memory. If you pulse the same three meters twice, the second pulse produces no new information. Track your grid mentally or mark cleared squares.

Costs ~5 pulses 📦

Sweeping the Dark Forest

The Dark Forest only holds coin caches — nothing that changes a run. Skip the sweep entirely. Save every pulse for the Graveyard's bone charm and the Mansion's statue.

⚖️ Buy a Battery vs Find One in Tung Tung Sahur

The truck shop sells batteries for 15 coins. The map hides three free ones. Both are valid ways to extend your pulse budget — but they serve different runs.

🛒

Buy from the Truck Shop

15 Coins
  • Instant — no travel time required in the Dark Forest
  • Available before the run starts at the truck
  • Costs one perfect loaf plus a rushed loaf
  • Best when you want to sweep the Graveyard aggressively
  • Pays for itself if it finds the bone charm
🗺️

Find on the Map

Free
  • No coin cost — keep your money for the lucky charm
  • Requires 15–25 seconds of travel per battery
  • Puts you on the map during the wooden kentongan's patrol loop
  • West Cabin battery is the easiest to grab safely
  • Best when you have plenty of time in the run

For most Tung Tung Sahur runs, the correct answer is buy one battery in the truck shop and pick up the West Cabin battery if the kentongan is nowhere near it. That gives you 70 total pulses for a cost of 15 coins and roughly 15 seconds of travel. The East Well and Village Well batteries are optional — grab them only if you already have the bone charm and the run is going well.

Do not buy two batteries. The lucky charm is worth 30 coins and it changes the run more than any number of extra pulses. Save your money for the charm and let the map batteries do the rest.

💡 Pulse Discipline in One Sentence

If you remember nothing else from this Tung Tung Sahur guide, remember this.

Every pulse you tap is a pulse you cannot tap again — and the Golden Statue only reveals itself in the final minutes. Tap like the run depends on it, because in Tung Tung Sahur it does.
