🏆 What the Golden Statue Is

A small golden statue shaped like Tung Tung Sahur himself. People in the game say it can make you rich. The statue is the only thing you are actually looking for. Bread, batteries, and relics are just the tools you use to find it.

The Golden Statue is buried in the Cursed Mansion, the fourth and final zone. It is not visible on the surface. It does not appear on the map. The metal detector points toward it only in the final minutes of a run. Before that, the detector ignores it entirely.

The statue is small enough to carry in one hand. It weighs almost nothing — but carrying it changes how you move. Your walk speed drops by 20%. Your crouch is disabled. You cannot hide behind low cover. Every door you opened on the way in is still open, and every corridor you cleared is now a threat again.

Digging up the statue takes 6 seconds. During those 6 seconds, you cannot move, cannot cancel the dig, and cannot defend yourself. The dig produces a continuous scraping sound that draws the patrol from up to 15 meters away. The only way to survive the dig is to start it when the patrol is at least two wings away — not just out of sight, but separated from you by at least one closed door.

📍 Where to Find It

The statue is buried in one of four wings of the Cursed Mansion. Which wing depends on a clue you find in the Abandoned Village. Without that clue, you must sweep all four wings with the detector.

🗺️

The Map Fragment

Found on the church altar in the Abandoned Village. Picking it up triggers the church bell, which pulls the patrol toward you on a 30-second timer. The fragment marks which wing of the Mansion holds the statue. Without it, you search blind.

🦴

The Bone Charm

Buried in the Graveyard's northwest quadrant. Once you have it, the Golden Statue glows through walls. You can see its location from the Mansion entrance without using a single detector pulse. This is the single most useful relic for finding the statue.

👂

The Hum

The statue emits a low, continuous hum. The hum is audible within 3 meters. If you have the bone charm and know the wing, you can find the statue without the metal detector at all — walk the wing and listen for the hum.

🔍

The Detector Signal

If you do not have the bone charm, the metal detector is your only option. The statue's signal only appears in the final 3 minutes of a run. The arrow swings in a slow circle rather than pointing. Sweep the wing in a grid, tapping the scan button every three steps.

⛏️ The Dig: What Happens in Those 6 Seconds

The dig is the most dangerous action in the game. Six seconds is long enough for the patrol to cross an entire wing. If you start the dig at the wrong moment, you will not finish it.

When you press the dig button, your character kneels down and begins scraping the floor. The animation is not cancellable. If you release the button, the dig resets and you must start over. You cannot move, cannot crouch, and cannot open doors during the dig.

The scrape produces a continuous sound. The patrol hears it from 15 meters away — roughly one wing's length. If the patrol is in the adjacent wing when you start digging, he will reach you before the dig finishes. If he is two wings away, you have about 2 seconds of buffer after the dig completes before he arrives. That buffer is your window to pick up the statue and move.

"The statue weighs nothing. But you will feel every step back to the truck."

In-game hint shown when the statue is first lifted

If you lose a life while carrying the statue, you drop it. The statue remains where it fell. You must return to that exact spot to pick it up again. If the patrol is between you and the statue, you may not be able to retrieve it before the run ends.

🏃 Carrying It Back: The Return Trip

The run does not end when you dig up the statue. It ends when you reach the truck. The return trip is longer than the trip in, because the statue slows you down and removes your crouch.

1

Movement Penalty

Carrying the statue reduces your walk speed by 20%. You cannot sprint. You cannot crouch. You move at a slow, steady pace. If the patrol sees you, you cannot outrun him. Your only option is to break line of sight and hope he loses you.

2

No Crouch

Crouching is disabled while carrying the statue. Low walls, crates, and furniture no longer provide cover. You must walk around them or through open spaces. This makes the Mansion's already narrow corridors even more dangerous.

3

Open Doors Stay Open

Every door you opened on the way in is still open. The patrol can use those same doors to follow you. If you opened a shortcut, you also created a shortcut for him. This is why experienced players open doors strategically — not all of them, only the ones they need.

4

The Truck

Reaching the truck ends the run immediately. You do not need to do anything else. The statue is placed in the truck automatically. The Golden Ending plays. Your run time is recorded. You can start a new run with the coins you earned.

🚫 Three Mistakes That End the Run at the Statue

These are the three behaviors that account for the highest number of failures in the final zone. All three happen at the statue itself — not on the way in, but at the moment of the dig.

⛏️

Digging Too Early

Starting the dig while the patrol is one wing away. The 6-second dig is longer than it feels. If he is adjacent, he will reach you before you finish. Always confirm he is two wings away — listen for footsteps, do not guess.

🚪

Opening Every Door on the Way In

Players open all doors to "save time later". But open doors let the patrol move faster too. Open only the doors you need for your planned return route. Leave the rest closed.

🏃

Panicking After the Dig

The moment the dig finishes, the urge is to grab the statue and sprint. But you cannot sprint. You walk. If you panic and run into a corridor the patrol is about to enter, you lose the life and the statue. Move deliberately. Follow your planned route.

💡 How to Plan Your Dig

The correct way to play the Mansion is to plan the dig before you enter the wing. This is the sequence that experienced players follow.

1️⃣

Get the Map Fragment First

Do not enter the Mansion without knowing which wing holds the statue. The fragment is in the Abandoned Village church. Pick it up, survive the bell, and enter the Mansion with a target wing in mind.

2️⃣

Find the Bone Charm

If you can find the bone charm in the Graveyard, take it. The charm lets you see the statue through walls. You can walk directly to the burial spot without using a single detector pulse. This saves 10 to 15 pulses and 2 to 3 minutes of searching.

3️⃣

Clear the Return Route First

Before you dig, walk the route back to the truck once. Open only the doors you need. Close every other door behind you. Count the seconds it takes. This is your escape plan. Do not improvise after the dig.

4️⃣

Listen Before You Dig

Stand at the burial spot and listen. The patrol's footsteps echo through the Mansion. If you can hear him clearly, he is in your wing. If you can barely hear him, he is two wings away. Dig when you can barely hear him.
