Metal Detector

The single most important tool in the game. 40 pulses per run. Each pulse tells you which direction to dig. Use them wrong, and the Cursed Mansion becomes a blind search with the patrol closing in. Use them right, and you find every relic you need without wasting a single scan.

🔍 What the Metal Detector Does

The detector finds buried relics. That is all it does. It does not find items on the surface. It does not find the patrol. It does not show you a map. It gives you a directional signal toward the nearest buried object. You follow the signal. You dig. That is the entire loop.

Every run gives you 40 pulses. One tap of the scan button equals one pulse. Holding the button drains the battery in about 15 seconds — meaning you get approximately one long scan instead of 40 short ones. The math is obvious: tap, do not hold.

Each pulse produces a directional indicator on the screen. The indicator points toward the nearest buried relic within range — about 25 meters. If there are multiple relics, the indicator points toward the closest one. If there are none in range, the indicator stays blank and the pulse is wasted. Nothing else happens. No sound, no visual effect. Just a wasted pulse.

The detector does not distinguish between relic types. A coin cache, a bone charm, a battery recharge, and the Golden Statue all produce the same signal. You only learn what you found after you dig it up. That is the point — you commit to a dig without knowing whether it is worth the time.

📡 Signal Types: What Each Relic Feels Like

The detector does not label relics. But the signal strength and pulse pattern vary subtly by relic type. Experienced players learn to tell them apart without digging. Here is what each one looks like.

💰

Coin Cache — Sharp Spike

Short, high-frequency signal. The arrow swings quickly from side to side. Dig time: 2 seconds. Value: 15 to 25 coins. Common in the Dark Forest and Graveyard.

🔋

Battery Recharge — Steady Pulse

Slow, regular signal. The arrow stays mostly still and points consistently. Dig time: 2 seconds. Effect: restores 15 pulses. Found in the Dark Forest's West Cabin and the Graveyard's East Well.

🦴

Bone Charm — Slow Throb

The slowest signal in the game. The arrow barely moves for the first 2 seconds, then pulses once. Only found in the Graveyard's northwest quadrant. Dig time: 4 seconds. The most valuable single relic in the game.

🏆

Golden Statue — Deep Hum

Only appears in the final 3 minutes of a run. The arrow swings in a slow circle rather than pointing. Accompanied by a low audible hum. Dig time: 6 seconds. The win condition.

🧮 The Pulse Budget: How to Spend 40 Scans

Forty pulses sound like a lot. They are not. If you spend them evenly across all four zones, you will run out before the Mansion. The correct approach is to save at least 10 for the final zone.

Zone Recommended Pulses What You're Looking For
🌲 Dark Forest0–5Skip most scans here. The zone has three coin caches. Not worth the pulses.
🪦 Graveyard10–15The bone charm. Sweep the northwest quadrant only. Find it, stop scanning.
🏚️ Abandoned Village5–10One battery recharge in the East Well. Optional if you already have 20+ pulses left.
🏰 Cursed Mansion10–15The Golden Statue. Save at least 10 pulses for the final dig.

If you buy two battery recharges (15 coins each), your total pulse budget becomes 70. That lets you scan the Dark Forest more freely. But batteries are optional — the correct play is usually to skip them and learn to scan efficiently.

🔋 Battery Management

The battery is your second-most-important resource after bread. Three rules keep you from wasting it.

1

Never Hold the Button

Holding the scan button drains the battery in 15 seconds. Tapping it gives you 40 pulses over the same period. The held scan covers a slightly wider area, but the trade-off is not worth it. Always tap.

2

Scan in a Grid, Not Randomly

Walk in a straight line, tap every 3 meters. Turn 90 degrees, walk back parallel, tap every 3 meters. This grid search covers ground efficiently. Random scans miss relics in the gaps.

3

Stop When You Find the Target

If you are looking for the bone charm in the Graveyard's northwest quadrant, stop scanning the moment you find it. Do not keep sweeping "just in case". Every pulse you save goes to the Mansion.

4

Buy Batteries Only if You Have Coins to Spare

A battery costs 15 coins. Three perfect loaves earn 36 coins. If you have baked well and have more than 40 coins, buy a battery. Otherwise, save the coins for the lucky charm.

🗺️ Grid Search Technique: The Correct Way to Sweep

Most players waste pulses by walking randomly and tapping whenever they feel like it. The correct technique is a systematic grid. It covers ground with fewer pulses and finds relics faster.

The grid is simple: choose an area — say the northwest quadrant of the Graveyard. Walk in a straight line from one edge to the other, tapping the scan button every three steps. When you reach the far edge, take two steps to the side, then walk back parallel to your first line, tapping every three steps. Repeat until you have covered the entire area.

Three steps between pulses is the optimal spacing. Two steps wastes pulses on overlapping ground. Four steps risks skipping the space between scan radii. The detector's effective radius is about 4 meters. Three steps (roughly 3 meters) ensures full coverage with minimal overlap.

"The detector does not tell you what is buried. It tells you what is close. The grid tells you where close begins."

Common community advice for new players

Do not scan areas you have already covered. The detector has a memory — if you scan the same ground twice, the second pulse is wasted. Track your grid mentally or use the in-game map marker system to mark cleared squares.

🚫 Three Mistakes That Waste Pulses

These are the three behaviors that account for the most wasted pulses across all players. Avoiding them saves you 10 to 20 pulses per run.

🔋

Holding the Button

The held scan covers a wider area but drains the battery in 15 seconds. If you hold it three times, you lose the entire battery. Tap instead. Always tap.

🎯

Scanning Without a Target

Do not walk into a zone and start scanning. Decide what you are looking for — coin cache, bone charm, battery — before you scan. Every pulse should have a purpose.

🔁

Double-Checking a Dig

After you dig up a relic, the detector stops pointing at that spot. There is nothing left there. Do not re-scan the same ground hoping for a second relic. Move on.

💡 Advanced Techniques

Once you have played ten or more runs, these advanced techniques can shave a minute or two off your time — and save lives in the Mansion.

👂

Listen for the Hum

The Golden Statue emits a low hum within 3 meters. You do not need the detector to find it if you are close enough. Listen for the hum before you waste a pulse.

🎯

Use the Bone Charm to Skip Scans

Once you have the bone charm, relics glow through walls. You can see the Golden Statue's location from the entrance of the Mansion. No pulses needed.

🔍

Scan at Ground Level, Not from Cover

The detector works from any position, but the directional indicator is easier to read when you are not crouched behind a tree. If you are scanning for the bone charm in the Graveyard, stand up briefly. Then crouch again.

⏱️

Pause Between Pulses

If you tap the scan button too fast, the directional indicator does not update between pulses. Wait 1 second between taps for the arrow to settle. This prevents wasted scans on already-detected relics.
