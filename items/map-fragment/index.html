Story Item

Map Fragment

A torn corner of paper with a hand-drawn sketch of the Cursed Mansion. It does not show you where the Golden Statue is buried. It shows you which of the four wings you need to search — and that single piece of information saves 20 detector pulses every run.

What It Looks Like

A palm-sized corner of aged paper. The edges are torn, not cut. The drawing is in faded brown ink — a rough rectangle with an X in one quadrant. The X marks which of the Mansion's four wings holds the Golden Statue.

The fragment is specific to the current run. A different run produces a different X. The paper looks the same, but the mark on it changes.

  • Not a metal detector target — it is on a surface, not buried
  • Not consumable — it stays in your inventory for the run
  • Not shareable — no two runs show the same wing
  • Not optional for a good run — skipping it doubles your Mansion search time

📜 What the Fragment Actually Does

Three things. That is the entire mechanical effect of the item.

First, it narrows your Mansion search to one of four wings. Without it, you have to sweep all four wings with the metal detector to find the statue's burial spot. With it, you can skip three wings entirely and go straight to the one marked with the X.

Second, it saves pulses. Sweeping three entire wings costs roughly 15 to 20 detector taps. That is a third of your total battery. The fragment does not cost any pulses to use — you just look at it and read the letter.

Third, it saves time. Each wing takes about 60 seconds to sweep carefully. Skipping three of them gives you roughly three extra minutes in the run. In a game where the patrol loop gets tighter the deeper you go, three minutes is often the difference between reaching the truck and losing a life on the way back.

🏰 The Four Wings Explained

The fragment marks one wing per run. These are the four possibilities and what each one changes about your route.

N

North Wing

Wide corridors with tall windows. Easiest to navigate, most exposed to sightlines. The key is in the study at the far end. If the fragment marks North, take the direct route — the wing has fewer hiding spots but shorter distances.

E

East Wing

A single long hallway with doors on both sides. Exposed and narrow. The key is behind the farthest door. If the fragment marks East, plan your exit before you enter — there is nowhere to hide in the corridor itself.

S

South Wing

Narrow corridors with many side rooms. Easiest to get lost in. The key is in a second-floor bedroom. If the fragment marks South, expect the longest search — the wing has more rooms to check before you reach the statue.

W

West Wing

Largest wing, with a grand staircase and a ballroom. Loudest floorboards in the Mansion. The key is under the staircase. If the fragment marks West, walk slowly — the creaking floor is the biggest risk here.

⛪ How to Get It: The Church Sequence

The fragment is on the church altar in the Abandoned Village. Reaching it triggers a four-step escape sequence that cannot be interrupted. Here is the exact order.

STEP 1 🚪

Enter the Courtyard

Walk through the stone threshold. The moment your feet cross the line, the church bell rings. The bell pulls the patrol toward the church — you have about 30 seconds.

STEP 2 📜

Grab the Fragment

The fragment sits on the altar. Picking it up takes 2 seconds. It does not need to be dug. It does not need the metal detector. Just walk up to it and press interact.

STEP 3 🏃

Choose Your Exit

Four routes leave the courtyard. South Alley is the safest. East Well is medium-length. West Fields is open ground. North Path runs into the patrol — never take it.

STEP 4 🌲

Reach the Graveyard Gate

Once you are out of the courtyard and past the first house, you are safe. The patrol returns to his normal loop after a 45-second cooldown. Do not stop running until you reach the tree line.

🔋 Pulses Saved: With vs Without

The fragment does not cost a single pulse. It saves them. This is the exact math on a typical Mansion run.

Without Fragment
20
Pulses Burned Searching

You sweep all four wings with the metal detector. Each wing takes about 5 pulses to check thoroughly. By the time you find the statue, you have almost no battery left for the return trip.

With Fragment
0
Pulses Burned Searching

You walk directly to the marked wing and dig. No sweeping needed. The 20 pulses you would have burned stay in the battery for emergencies, extra coin caches, or a final re-scan near the truck.

Two things make this saving more valuable than it first appears. First, the Mansion is the last zone in the run, so every pulse you save is a pulse you keep for the highest-stakes part of the game. Second, the return trip with the statue in your hands is slow and exposes you to the patrol. Having 20 spare pulses for a final safety scan — or to locate a coin cache on the way out — can be the difference between a lost run and a finished one.

🗺️ Fragment vs Church Clue

Two items sit on the same church altar. They are different and they do different things. Here is how to tell them apart.

Item Location Effect Required for Secret Ending?
📜 Map Fragment Church altar (Abandoned Village) Reveals which wing holds the statue No — but skipping it makes the Mansion much harder
📄 Church Clue Church pew (Abandoned Village) Lore only — explains why the statue was buried No — pure story item, no gameplay change

The easy way to remember the difference: the fragment is on the altar, the clue is on a pew. If you only have time to grab one before the bell timer runs out, grab the fragment. The clue is nice for story, but the fragment is a real gameplay advantage.

⚙️ How It Works With Other Items

Two interactions that matter. Everything else is unrelated.

🦴

Bone Charm

Redundant with the fragment. The bone charm shows every buried relic through walls — including the statue. If you already have the charm, the fragment is not strictly necessary. But if you are missing the charm, the fragment becomes the single most valuable item in the run.

🔋

Detector Battery

Complementary. The fragment saves 20 pulses. A battery restores 15. Combined, these two items give you roughly 35 extra pulses of search capability in the Mansion — more than enough to find every relic in the zone even without the bone charm.
