Church Clue

The most missable item in Tung Tung Sahur. It sits on a church pew in the Abandoned Village, right next to the map fragment on the altar. Most players grab the fragment and run before the bell timer kills them — they never look down at the pew. But the church clue is the only item in the game that explains why the Golden Statue was buried in the first place.

📜 What the Church Clue Is

A torn page from an old journal, folded and left on a pew. It has no gameplay effect. It does not open doors, find relics, or help you escape. It is a piece of story — and it is the only piece of story in Tung Tung Sahur that explains the Golden Statue.

Church Clue · Torn Journal Page

We buried it at dusk. The priests said it would bring fortune to whoever found it. The priests said it would bring ruin to whoever kept it. The priests said many things. They were not the ones carrying the shovel.

We chose the mansion because no one walks there anymore. We chose the east-facing room because the morning sun hits it first. We chose the lock because the key fits into the west staircase — and we kept the key.

The statue is small. You can carry it in one hand. You will wish it weighed nothing.

— an unnamed villager

The clue is not required for any ending in Tung Tung Sahur. You can finish the Golden Ending, the Bad Ending, and the Secret Ending without ever picking it up. But if you pick it up, the run stops being about finding a statue and starts being about understanding why the statue exists at all.

The clue sits on a back pew in the Abandoned Village church, four or five rows from the altar. It does not glow, does not sparkle, and does not appear on the metal detector. It looks like part of the environment — a piece of paper that has been there for a hundred years. You have to physically walk to the pew and press interact. Most players never do, because the church bell has already rung and they are sprinting for the exit.

🔍 How to Find It

Four steps. The clue is on the way to the altar — but it is not on the altar. That is the trap.

1

Enter the Church Courtyard

The bell rings the moment your foot crosses the stone threshold. You have about 30 seconds before the wooden kentongan redirects toward the church. Do not panic — the clue is inside the church, not outside.

2

Walk Past the Altar First

The map fragment is on the altar. Grab it — that is your priority. The fragment tells you which wing of the Cursed Mansion holds the Golden Statue. The clue does not.

3

Check the Back Pew on Your Way Out

On your way to the exit, glance at the pews on your left. The clue sits on a back pew, folded flat. It takes 1 second to pick up. If you already have the fragment and you are heading for the door, this costs almost nothing.

4

Read It After You Escape

You can read the clue from your inventory at any point in the run. You do not need to read it in the church. Grab it, run, and read it when you are safely in the Graveyard or back at the truck.

The clue is on the pew, not the altar. That is the trap. Players who enter the church in Tung Tung Sahur are conditioned to look at the altar because the map fragment is there. They grab the fragment and run. The clue is two meters behind them, sitting on a pew they walked past on the way in.

🧠 Three Facts the Clue Reveals

The clue is short, but it changes three things about how you understand the game. None of these are stated anywhere else in Tung Tung Sahur.

Fact 01

The Statue Was Buried by Villagers

The Golden Statue was not hidden by a king, a cult, or a monster. It was buried by ordinary villagers — the same people who lived in the Abandoned Village before it was abandoned. They buried it "at dusk" with shovels and a decision.

Fact 02

The Priests Warned Them

The villagers were told the statue would bring "fortune to whoever found it" and "ruin to whoever kept it." They buried it anyway. The implication is that the statue was not a treasure to them — it was a problem to be hidden.

Fact 03

The Key Was Kept

The villagers locked the mansion door and kept the key. That key is the Mansion Key III, hidden under the grand staircase in the West Wing. The clue connects the Abandoned Village to the Cursed Mansion in a way no other item does.

None of these facts are stated anywhere else. The map fragment tells you where the statue is. The church clue tells you why it is there. Together, they are the only two items in Tung Tung Sahur that connect the Abandoned Village to the Cursed Mansion as a single story instead of two separate zones.

⚖️ Church Clue vs Map Fragment

Both items sit in the same church in Tung Tung Sahur. Both are picked up in the same 30-second window. But they do completely different things.

Feature 📜 Map Fragment 📄 Church Clue
Location Church altar Church back pew
Gameplay effect Reveals which Mansion wing holds the statue None — lore only
Required for any ending? No, but skipping it makes the Mansion much harder No — fully optional
Missable? No — it is on the altar you have to visit Yes — most players walk past it
Reading time Instant — the X is visible on pickup 15 seconds — you have to read the page

If you only have time to grab one item in the church in Tung Tung Sahur, grab the map fragment. It has a direct gameplay effect. The church clue is for players who have already beaten the game once and want to understand what they were actually doing. It is the difference between finishing the run and understanding the run.

💡 One Reason to Pick It Up

If you have already beaten Tung Tung Sahur once, the clue is the only new thing left to find in the Abandoned Village.

Every other item in the church is functional. The map fragment is functional. The bell timer is functional. The exit routes are functional. The church clue is the only item in the Abandoned Village that exists purely to tell you a story — and it is the only item that connects the village you are running through to the mansion you are running toward.

Pick it up on your next run. Read it in the Graveyard. It takes one second to grab and fifteen seconds to read, and it changes how the whole game feels.
