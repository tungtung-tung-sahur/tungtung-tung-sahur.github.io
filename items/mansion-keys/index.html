Mansion Keys

In the Cursed Mansion of Tung Tung Sahur, three keys are hidden across four wings. Without them, doors stay locked — and forcing a lock makes noise that pulls the wooden kentongan straight toward you. Here is where each key is, which door it opens, and why finding them all before you dig is the difference between a win and a restart.

🔑 The Three Keys at a Glance

Three keys. Four wings. One exit. Every key in the Cursed Mansion of Tung Tung Sahur has a specific door it opens — and every door you leave locked is a corridor you cannot use on the return trip.

The Cursed Mansion in Tung Tung Sahur is the final zone. It has four wings — North, South, East, and West — and the Golden Statue is buried in one of them. Three of those wings hold a key. The East Wing does not. Every key opens a specific locked door somewhere else in the Mansion, and every locked door is a shortcut you need on the way back to the truck with the statue in your hands.

Most players enter the Mansion, scan for the statue, dig it up, and then discover that the return route is blocked by a locked door they never bothered to open. That is where runs end. Not at the dig. On the way back, carrying a statue that slows you down while the kentongan patrols the corridor you cannot pass through.

The correct order is: find every key you can, open every door on your planned return route, then and only then dig up the statue. Keys are not optional. They are the difference between an open path and a dead end.

🏰 Which Wing Holds Which Key

Four wings. Three keys. One wing has no key at all — knowing which one saves you a wasted sweep in Tung Tung Sahur.

N Key I · Study
E No Key · Skip
S Key II · Bedroom
W Key III · Staircase

The East Wing is the only wing without a key. It is also the longest and most exposed corridor in the Mansion — a single hallway with doors on both sides and no side rooms to hide in. If you enter the East Wing in Tung Tung Sahur, you are doing it for one reason only: the Golden Statue happens to be there. Otherwise, skip it entirely.

The other three wings each hold one key. North has Key I in the study at the far end. South has Key II in the upstairs bedroom. West has Key III under the grand staircase. All three keys are on surfaces — they are not buried, and the metal detector does not find them. You have to physically walk to the right spot and pick them up.

📊 Key → Door → Risk

Each key opens a specific door. Here is what each one unlocks, why you need it, and what happens if you skip it.

Key Location Opens Why It Matters
🗝️ Key I North Wing · Study North Wing shortcut door Lets you bypass the central corridor on the return trip
🔑 Key II South Wing · Upstairs Bedroom South Wing rear exit Creates a second route back to the truck if the main one is blocked
🔐 Key III West Wing · Under Staircase West Wing ballroom door Connects the West Wing to the central hallway directly

If you are only going to grab one key in Tung Tung Sahur, grab the one that opens the door closest to the wing where the Golden Statue is buried. That is the door you will need on the way back. The other two keys are nice to have but optional — they open alternate routes, not the only route.

The mistake is grabbing all three keys on the way in and forgetting to actually open the doors. A key in your pocket does nothing. You have to walk to the locked door and use it. Mark the door location mentally before you dig up the statue. If you dig first and then try to find the door, the kentongan will already be halfway through his return loop.

⚖️ Find the Key vs Force the Lock

Every locked door in the Cursed Mansion can be forced. The question is whether the four seconds you save is worth the noise you make in Tung Tung Sahur.

🔑

Find the Key

Silent
  • Costs 10–20 seconds of walking
  • Produces zero noise — the kentongan never reacts
  • The door stays unlocked for the return trip
  • You can open it slowly, without panic
  • Best when you have time and the patrol is far
🔨

Force the Lock

Loud
  • Takes 4 seconds of standing still
  • Produces a loud bang heard from 25 meters away
  • Pulls the wooden kentongan straight to your position
  • Cannot be cancelled once started
  • Only worth it if the patrol is in the opposite wing

Forcing a lock is a gamble. In the best case, the kentongan is two wings away and the 4-second bang does not reach him. In the worst case, he is in the adjacent corridor, hears the bang, and reaches you before you can move. There is no partial success — you either get away with it or you lose a life.

Finding the key is slower but safe. It costs you 10 to 20 seconds, but it produces zero noise and it leaves the door unlocked for the entire run. On a return trip with the statue in your hands, that unlocked door is the difference between a clean exit and a dead end.

🚨 Three Mistakes That End Runs

All three of these happen in the Cursed Mansion of Tung Tung Sahur. All three are avoidable.

⛏️

Digging Before the Doors Are Open

  • You carry the statue, slow, with a locked door ahead
  • The kentongan is behind you, closing the gap
  • You cannot crouch — the statue disables it
  • You cannot outrun him — the statue slows you 20%
  • The run ends at the door, not at the dig
🚪

Opening Every Door on the Way In

  • Every open door is a shortcut the kentongan can also use
  • The Mansion becomes a straight line instead of a maze
  • He reaches you faster on the return trip
  • Open only the doors on your planned exit route
  • Leave the rest closed — they slow him down too
