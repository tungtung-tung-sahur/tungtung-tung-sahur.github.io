Bone Charm

The single most valuable relic in the game. It reveals buried treasure through walls — every zone you enter after finding it becomes an open map. Finding it is not the hard part. Getting back out of the Graveyard with it is.

🦴 What the Bone Charm Is

A small bone fragment on a leather cord. It does not have a visible description in the game. It does not appear in the shop. It exists for exactly one reason: to turn the metal detector into a wall-piercing scanner.

The Bone Charm is a permanent relic. You find it once in the Graveyard, and from that moment on, every buried relic in every zone you have not yet completed shows up as a faint glowing shape through walls. The Golden Statue. The coin caches. The battery recharges. Everything. You do not need to scan for them. You just see them.

This changes the Cursed Mansion more than any other item in the game. Without the charm, the Mansion is a blind sweep — you burn 15 to 20 detector pulses guessing which wing the statue is buried in. With the charm, you can stand at the entrance of the Mansion and see exactly which room the statue is in. The charm does not just save pulses. It saves entire runs.

The charm is also the single most common reason players lose their best runs. Most deaths in the Graveyard happen while digging up the charm. The wooden character moves faster in that zone, the fog hides him until he is almost on top of you, and the dig takes a full four seconds during which you cannot move. Finding the charm is not the hard part. Getting back out with it is.

"The bone charm does not show you what is buried. It shows you what was buried — outlines through walls. Every relic in every zone becomes a faint glowing shape. Every statue. Every cache. Every key. You do not need the detector anymore. You just need eyes."

In-game description of the bone charm

👁️ Vision: With vs Without

This is what the charm actually does. Two side-by-side examples of the same Mansion hallway — one with the charm equipped, one without.

Without Charm
🚪

Blank Walls

Walls, doors, and floor. Nothing glows. You need to scan every 3 meters with the metal detector to find buried relics — and you will not know if you missed one until the run ends.

With Charm
✨

Glowing Outlines

Faint green and gold outlines pulse through the walls. The statue shows as a tall vertical shape. Caches show as small circles. Keys show as short horizontal dashes. You see all of them at once.

The outlines only appear for relics you have not yet collected. Once you dig up a relic, its outline disappears and does not come back. The charm does not show you where the wooden character is, where the exits are, or where items you already own are sitting. It shows only buried relics that are still in the ground.

The visual effect is subtle. It is not a bright overlay — it is a faint, translucent shimmer that pulses slowly. On bright screens or in a well-lit room, it can be almost invisible. On a phone at night, it is obvious. If you are playing during the day and cannot see the outlines, turn the in-game brightness down slightly. The contrast improves dramatically.

🗺️ Where It Is Buried

The charm is always in the same quadrant of the Graveyard, but the exact grave varies between runs. Four quadrants, one of them holds the charm. You can cross the other three off immediately.

NW — Check Here The Bone Charm is always in this quadrant. One of three graves.
NE Coin caches only. Skip unless you need coins.
SW Nothing buried. Used only as a route to the exit.
SE Battery recharge. Useful if you burned too many pulses.

⏱️ How to Find It: Four-Step Timeline

The charm takes about 90 seconds to find if you know what you are doing. Here is the exact sequence that works.

Step 1 · 10 seconds

Enter through the north gate, stay left

Walk slowly, crouch the entire time. Do not scan yet. The wooden character starts his loop at the north gate, so you have about five seconds of safety before he begins moving south.

Step 2 · 20 seconds

Cross to the northwest quadrant

Stay along the western tree line. Do not cross the central tomb. When you reach the northwest corner, you are outside the character's main patrol path — he rarely crosses this far west. This is the safest place in the zone.

Step 3 · 40 seconds

Pulse-sweep the three graves

There are exactly three graves in the NW quadrant. Tap the detector once at each grave. The charm produces a distinctive slow throb signal — different from the sharp coin spike and different from the steady battery pulse. When you hear the throb, stop and dig.

Step 4 · 4 seconds + escape

Dig and get out

The dig takes 4 seconds. Start it only when you cannot hear the character's footsteps — not when you cannot see him, but when you cannot hear him. Once the charm is in hand, move south immediately. Do not scan anything else in the Graveyard. The charm is the only reason you are here.

🆚 Charm vs Detector: What Each Finds

The charm does not replace the detector — it extends it. Here is what each tool can and cannot find.

🦴 Bone Charm Yes — slow throb signal N/A (you already have it)
💰 Coin Cache Yes — sharp spike Yes — small circle outline
🔋 Battery Recharge Yes — steady pulse Yes — medium oval outline
🏆 Golden Statue Yes — only in final 3 minutes Yes — immediately visible
🗺️ Map Fragment No — story item, not buried No — story item, not buried
🔑 Mansion Keys No — keys are on surfaces Yes — horizontal dash outline

🚨 Three Ways Players Lose the Charm

Almost every failed charm attempt happens for one of three reasons. All three are avoidable with one small change to your routine.

⛏️

Digging Too Early

The four-second dig is longer than it feels. If you can still hear the character's footsteps clearly, he is close. Wait until the footsteps fade to a distant throb before starting the dig.

🌫️

Trusting the Fog

The fog hides him from you, not you from him. He can still see you at 15 meters through the fog. If you crouch and listen, you are safe. If you walk upright and hope for the best, you are not.

🚪

Opening the Gate

The Graveyard's north gate produces a loud sound when opened. If you open it while the character is patrolling nearby, he redirects toward you immediately. Always wait for him to pass the gate before entering or leaving.
