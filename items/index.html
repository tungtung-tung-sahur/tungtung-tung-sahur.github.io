Items of Tung Tung Tung Sahur

Every tool, relic, key, and consumable in the game. The metal detector that finds buried treasure. The Golden Statue that ends the run. The wooden bat you never use. The bread that pays for everything else.

🎒 Tools

Tools are permanent equipment. You start with them. They do not break. They do not need to be found. They define how you play.

🔍 Tool

Metal Detector

40 pulses per run

Your primary search tool. Finds buried relics, the bone charm, and the Golden Statue. Short taps preserve the battery. Holding the button drains it in 15 seconds.

Full guide →
🏏 Tool

Wooden Bat

Carried, never used

The character carries it. You never swing it. It is part of the visual identity of Tung Tung Sahur, but in Baker Treasure Hunt it stays on your back for the entire run.

Full guide →
🔋 Consumable

Detector Battery

Restores 15 pulses

Found in the Graveyard's East Well and the Dark Forest's West Cabin. Each battery restores 15 of the 40 detector pulses. Two batteries per run is standard.

Full guide →

🏆 Relics

Relics are permanent upgrades. You find them once. They change how the rest of the run plays. Some are optional. One is the win condition.

🏆 Relic

Golden Statue

Final win condition

Buried in the Cursed Mansion. Digging it up takes 6 seconds. Carrying it back to the truck slows you by 20% and removes your crouch. The only way to win.

Full guide →
🦴 Relic

Bone Charm

Reveals treasure through walls

Buried in the Graveyard's northwest quadrant. Once found, every buried relic in every zone becomes a faint glowing shape through walls. Changes the Mansion from blind digging to planned digging.

Full guide →
🍀 Relic

Lucky Charm

Fourth life for next run

Bought from the truck shop for 30 coins. Does not apply to the current run. Applies to your next attempt. One of the most useful purchases for players learning the Cursed Mansion.

Full guide →

💰 Currency

There is only one currency in the game. It is not gold. It is not coins. It is bread. Bread pays for tools, charms, and upgrades.

🥖 Currency

Ramadan Bread

5 to 12 coins per loaf

Baked in the truck oven. Three ingredients spawn near the truck each run. A perfect loaf earns 12 coins. A rushed loaf earns 5. A burnt loaf earns 2. The timing window is roughly 4 seconds.

Full guide →
🌾 Currency

Bread Ingredients

3 per run

Three ingredients spawn near the truck in the Dark Forest. One is always within 30 meters. The other two are further out — near the east cabin and the north watchtower. Collecting all three before baking is optional but efficient.

Full guide →
💰 Currency

Coin Cache

15 to 25 coins

Buried in the Dark Forest and the Graveyard. The metal detector finds it. A quick source of coins if you need to buy a battery or a lucky charm. Not worth a full sweep — the bread economy is more reliable.

Full guide →

🔑 Keys & Story Items

Keys open locked doors. Story items reveal where the Golden Statue is buried. Without them, the final zone is a blind search.

🔑 Key

Mansion Keys

3 keys · 4 wings

Three keys are scattered across the four wings of the Cursed Mansion. Each opens a different locked door. Forcing a lock takes 4 seconds and produces a loud bang. Find the keys instead.

Full guide →
🗺️ Story Item

Map Fragment

Reveals statue quadrant

Found on the church altar in the Abandoned Village. The church bell rings the moment you enter the courtyard. The fragment marks which wing of the Cursed Mansion holds the Golden Statue. Without it, you must sweep all four wings.

Full guide →
📜 Story Item

Church Clue

Optional lore item

A torn page found in the church alongside the map fragment. Does not affect gameplay. Explains why the Golden Statue was buried and who hid it. Missable if you leave the church before picking it up.

⚙️ How Items Work Together

The game has a simple item economy. Bread pays for batteries. Batteries find relics. Relics make the Mansion survivable. Every item is connected to every other item.

🥖→💰

Bread Pays for Everything

Three perfect loaves earn 36 coins. A detector battery costs 15. A lucky charm costs 30. If you bake well, you can afford both upgrades before entering the Graveyard.

🔋→🔍

Batteries Extend Your Search

Each battery restores 15 pulses. Two batteries per run give you 70 total pulses instead of 40. That is enough to find the bone charm and still have 10 pulses left for the Mansion.

🦴→🏆

The Bone Charm Saves the Mansion

Without the bone charm, you dig blind in the Mansion. With it, every buried relic — including the Golden Statue — glows through walls. The charm costs 10 to 15 pulses to find. It saves you 20 pulses later.

💡 Item Priority for New Players

If you are on your first five runs, this is the order you should focus on items. Everything else is secondary.

1️⃣

Bake Three Perfect Loaves

Before you explore, bake. Three perfect loaves give you 36 coins. That is enough for a battery and a lucky charm. Do not spend coins on anything else early.

2️⃣

Buy a Battery Before the Graveyard

The Graveyard has the bone charm. The bone charm takes 10 to 15 pulses to find. If you enter with only 20 pulses left, you will not have enough to search the Mansion afterward.

3️⃣

Skip the Wooden Bat

The bat is not a usable item. It is part of the character design. Do not look for a way to swing it. Do not wait for an attack button. It does not exist.
