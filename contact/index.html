📋 Message Guidelines

Two short lists — what helps us help you, and what slows down the whole process.

✅ What Helps

  • Include the exact page URL when reporting a problem
  • Describe what you expected vs what actually happened
  • Mention your device and browser for technical issues
  • Quote the exact text if you are reporting a correction
  • Suggest a specific fix when you have one in mind
  • Keep the subject line specific and descriptive

🚫 What Slows Things Down

  • Sending the same message multiple times
  • Asking questions that are on the FAQ page
  • Vague reports like "it does not work" with no details
  • Attaching large files that are not requested
  • Reporting bugs in other games on this fan site
  • Using the form for advertising or promotion
