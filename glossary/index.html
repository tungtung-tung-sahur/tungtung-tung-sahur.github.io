A 3 terms
Almond Water Mechanic
A consumable in Backrooms Escape that restores approximately 50% of the sanity meter. Also appears in some Tung Tung Sahur horror games as a rare item. Named after the community creepypasta staple of the same name.
Anomaly Meme
The original Indonesian term used in the first TikTok video to describe Tung Tung Sahur: "anomali mengerikan" (scary anomaly). Fans often call the character "the anomaly" as a shorthand, especially in the horror games.
Avocadini Guffo Character
An avocado-themed brainrot character from the extended universe. Appears in Steal a Brainrot as a mid-tier collectible. Not to be confused with Chimpanzini Ananasini.
B 5 terms
Ballerina Cappuccina Character
A ballerina with a cappuccino cup for a head. Created by a Romanian TikTok user in March 2025. Appears as a Legendary-tier unit in Steal a Brainrot. Her Fortnite skin became the lowest-rated cosmetic in the game's history. Full profile →
Brainrot Culture
Internet slang describing content considered low-quality, absurd, or mind-numbing, especially short-form videos. The term comes from "brain rot" — the sensation of your mind turning to mush after consuming too much low-effort content. The Tung Tung Sahur universe is a core part of the "Italian brainrot" subgenre.
Brainrot God Culture
A rarity tier in Steal a Brainrot above Mythic. Characters at this tier cost $10 million and generate $50,000 per second. Tralalero Tralala is the most famous Brainrot God unit.
Bombardiro Crocodilo Character
A crocodile fused with a World War II bomber. First appeared in February 2025. One of the earliest Italian brainrot characters and a Mythic-tier unit in Steal a Brainrot. Full profile →
Brr Brr Patapim Character
A proboscis monkey fused with a tree, wearing a golden hat with a blue frog named Slim inside it. Created by @ofuscabreno in February 2025. Famous for the powerscaled battle where he crushes Tung Tung Sahur beneath his feet. Full profile →
C 5 terms
Capybarello Cocosini Character
A capybara inside a coconut shell. Described in lore as a divine capybara god from the beaches of Bali. Was the first Legendary-tier brainrot in Steal a Brainrot. Created by @alexey_pigeon. Full profile →
Chimpanzini Bananini Character
A green chimpanzee whose lower half is inside a banana peel. The first character in @alexey_pigeon's lineup of monkey-fruit hybrids. Appears in Toilet Tower Defense and Steal a Brainrot. Full profile →
Church Bell Lore
A scripted event in the Abandoned Village. The bell rings once when you enter the church courtyard, giving you roughly 30 seconds before the wooden character redirects his patrol toward the sound. Cannot be stopped — only planned around.
Cocofanto Elefanto Character
An elephant with a coconut. Described in fan lore as Capybarello Cocosini's best friend. A Brainrot God-tier character in Steal a Brainrot.
Crouch Mechanic
The safest stance in every Tung Tung Sahur game. Crouching does not make you quieter — the character cannot hear you at all — but it removes you from his visual detection cone behind partial cover. Essential for moving through open areas.
D 2 terms
Dig (Statue Dig) Mechanic
The final action before the win condition. Digging up the Golden Statue takes exactly 6 seconds. During those 6 seconds, you cannot move, cannot cancel, and produce a scraping sound that draws the character from up to 15 meters. The dig is the most dangerous single action in the game.
Doppelganger Character
An entity in the mobile version of Backrooms Escape that wears your face and mimics human movement to lure you in. Represented as a "deadly" threat.
E 1 term
Espressona Signora Character
Ballerina Cappuccina's sister. Another coffee-themed character in the extended brainrot family. The two are often shown together in family-themed fan content.
F 2 terms
Frigo Camelo Buffofardelle Character
A camel that doubles as a refrigerator. Created by @ofuscabreno a week after Boneca Ambalabu. In fan lore, he is Boneca's rival. Featured in powerscaled battles where he fights Boneca Ambalabu.
Fortnite Skin Culture
In April 2026, Epic Games added Tung Tung Sahur and Ballerina Cappuccina as cosmetic skins in Fortnite through an official partnership with Mementum Lab. The bundle includes a baseball bat pickaxe. The skin sparked controversy among players who opposed the use of AI-generated memes in the game.
G 3 terms
Golden Statue Lore
The final win condition in Baker Treasure Hunt. A small golden statue buried in the Cursed Mansion. Digging it up takes 6 seconds and carrying it back to the truck slows you by 20% while removing your crouch. Without the bone charm, the statue only appears on the metal detector in the final minutes of a run. Full item profile →
Gorillo Watermellondrillo Character
A gorilla-watermelon hybrid. One of the most popular monkey-fruit characters after Chimpanzini Bananini. Often described as Bananini's brother in fan lore.
Graveyard Fog Mechanic
A zone-wide hazard in the Graveyard level. Reduces visibility to roughly 8 meters for both you and the wooden character. The trade-off is that the Graveyard holds the bone charm, the single most valuable relic in the game.
H 3 terms
Hide-and-Seek Horror Mechanic
The genre label applied to Tung Tung Sahur and similar stealth games. Combines the structure of a children's hiding game with the tension of a horror chase. The defining feature is that the player cannot fight back — only hide, observe, and wait.
Hound Character
A quadruped entity in Backrooms Escape. Moderately dangerous, cannot open doors, and is faster than the player. Can be stunned by shining the flashlight directly into its eyes.
Human Authorship Culture
The legal doctrine at the center of the Tung Tung Sahur lawsuit. U.S. copyright law requires "human authorship" for a work to be protectable. Spyder Games argues that because Tung Tung Sahur originated from a DALL-E image, it fails this requirement. Mementum Lab argues the human creative elements — prompts, naming, backstory — qualify.
I 1 term
Italian Brainrot Culture
A subgenre of internet memes featuring absurd AI-generated characters with fake Italian names and voiceovers. Despite the name, most creators are not Italian. The genre includes Tralalero Tralala, Bombardiro Crocodilo, Ballerina Cappuccina, and Tung Tung Sahur.
K 1 term
Kentongan Lore
A traditional Indonesian slit drum used to wake people for sahur during Ramadan. The character Tung Tung Sahur is an anthropomorphic kentongan. The drum is struck with a wooden stick — which the character replaced with a baseball bat.
L 2 terms
Lirili Larila Character
An elephant wearing sandals, often described as standing in a desert. One of the top five most recognizable Italian brainrot characters. Frequently paired with Tung Tung Sahur in fan content.
Lucky Charm Lore
A purchasable upgrade in Baker Treasure Hunt. Costs 30 coins. Grants a fourth life on your next run. Does not apply to the current run. Worth buying if you are still learning the Cursed Mansion. Full item list →
M 3 terms
Mementum Lab Culture
A France-based licensing agency that acquired rights to Tung Tung Sahur from Noxa. Filed the cease-and-desist that removed the character from Steal a Brainrot in September 2025. Later filed counterclaims against Spyder Games.
Metal Detector Mechanic
The primary search tool in Baker Treasure Hunt. 40 pulses per run. Each tap produces a directional indicator toward the nearest buried relic. Holding the button drains the battery in 15 seconds — always tap, never hold. Full item guide →
Bombombini Gusini Character
A goose fused with a fighter jet. Described in fan lore as Bombardiro Crocodilo's brother or rival. Both are aerial characters. Fans depict them in conflict or as a team.
N 1 term
Noxa Culture
The Indonesian TikTok creator behind Tung Tung Sahur. Real name Fernanda Bagas Indrastata. He posted the first clip in February 2025 using DALL-E to generate the initial image. He later licensed the character to Mementum Lab. Full story →
O 1 term
OG Tier Mechanic
The rarest tier in Steal a Brainrot, above Secret. Only two characters currently hold this tier: Skibidi Toilet and Headless Horseman. Skibidi Toilet costs $300 billion and generates $330 million per second.
P 3 terms
Patrol Loop Mechanic
The fixed walking route the wooden character follows in every zone. Loops range from 15 seconds in the Cursed Mansion to 40 seconds in the Dark Forest. Learning the loop is the single most important skill in the game. Full stealth guide →
Penguino Cocosino Character
A penguin inside a coconut. Created by @alexey_pigeon days after Capybarello Cocosini. Part of the coconut-themed brainrot wave.
Public Domain (Status) Lore
The legal designation fans most commonly apply to Tung Tung Sahur. Under U.S. copyright law, works generated by AI without sufficient human authorship are not protectable. Because the character originated from a DALL-E image, it is considered unprotectable — though the lawsuit is testing this interpretation.
R 2 terms
Ramadan Lore
The Islamic holy month of fasting. The character Tung Tung Sahur exists because of a Ramadan tradition: the kentongan drum wakes people for sahur, the pre-dawn meal before fasting. The first TikTok clip was posted the day before Ramadan began in Indonesia.
Rebirth Mechanic
A prestige system in Steal a Brainrot. Rebirth resets your money and units but grants permanent income multipliers and extra base slots. There are 17 Rebirth levels in total, each requiring more money than the last.
S 5 terms
Sahur Lore
The pre-dawn meal eaten by Muslims before fasting during Ramadan. In Indonesia, communities traditionally wake each other for sahur by striking a kentongan drum. The character's full name is a direct reference to this tradition.
Sanity Meter Mechanic
A stat used in Backrooms Escape instead of a lives system. Drains slowly on most levels. Below 15% causes hallucinations — a warning sign. At zero, instant death. Restored primarily by drinking Almond Water.
Skin-Stealer Character
A "deadly" tier entity in Backrooms Escape. Repelled by light, cannot open doors, and forces the player to hide under beds or in closets. Not to be confused with the Doppelganger, which mimics the player's appearance.
Smiler Character
A moderately dangerous entity in Backrooms Escape. Stays in darkness and cannot be outrun. The counter is to stay under bright lights — the Smiler will not enter a lit area.
Steal a Brainrot Culture
A Roblox multiplayer tycoon game where players collect Italian brainrot characters, build bases, and steal from each other. Reached over 24 million concurrent players — a record for a single game on Roblox. The removal of Tung Tung Sahur triggered the current lawsuit. Full guide →
T 5 terms
Tralalero Tralala Character
A three-legged shark wearing blue Nike sneakers. Widely considered the first Italian brainrot character. Started the entire trend in January 2025. A Brainrot God-tier unit in Steal a Brainrot. Full profile →
Triple T Meme
A community abbreviation for Tung Tung Tung Sahur. Used primarily by English-speaking fans who find the full name too long for casual conversation. Not used in the original Indonesian source material.
Tung Tung God Meme
A meme format that depicts Tung Tung Sahur as a divine being with angel wings and a white body. Popular across TikTok and YouTube. The joke is treating an absurd meme character with religious reverence.
Tung Tung Tung Sahur Culture
An AI-generated internet character created by Indonesian TikTok user Noxa. Anthropomorphic kentongan with a baseball bat. Original video surpassed 120 million views. Subject of an unresolved copyright dispute. Full profile →
Tung Tung Tokens Mechanic
A currency used in the 3D Horror mobile game to permanently unlock anomalies. Introduced in the May 2025 content update alongside 15 new anomaly characters.
W 2 terms
Walid Character
One of 15 anomalies added to the 3D Horror version in May 2025. Part of the second wave of brainrot characters. Not to be confused with Walid ibn al-Mughira, the historical figure.
Wretch Mechanic
A moderately dangerous entity in Backrooms Escape. Appears on Levels 6 and 9. Crouching and sneaking is the primary counter. On Level 6, it appears as a red dot on the LIDAR scanner.
