🎬 What Makes a Tung Tung Sahur Meme

Every viral Tung Tung Sahur edit shares four elements. Remove any one of them and the meme stops working. These are the building blocks that made the character recognizable across millions of videos.

The Signature Sound

The "tung tung tung" rhythm is not background music. It mimics a traditional Indonesian kentongan drum being struck — the exact rhythm used to wake people for sahur during Ramadan. Without this audio, the meme loses its context.

The Wooden Body

Tung Tung Sahur is an anthropomorphic wooden figure. His body resembles a hollow slit drum, his eyes are wide and unblinking, and his limbs are thin and cartoonish. The design is deliberately absurd — a drum with a face, carrying a baseball bat.

The Baseball Bat

Historically, kentongans are struck with a wooden mallet. The meme replaced the mallet with a baseball bat. The bat is not a weapon in most edits — it is the tool that produces the sound. He strikes himself, or a door, or the ground.

The Sahur Lore

The character exists because of sahur, the pre-dawn meal eaten before fasting during Ramadan. The original voiceover warns: if someone is called for sahur three times and does not answer, Tung Tung Sahur comes to their house.

⏳ Meme Evolution Timeline

From a single Ramadan-themed TikTok clip to a Fortnite skin and a federal lawsuit. These are the key moments that shaped the meme.

February 2025 — First Appearance

The Original Clip

Indonesian TikTok creator Noxa (@noxaasht) posts the first Tung Tung Sahur video. A wooden figure taps at a door and calls out for sahur in a distorted voice. The clip is short, strange, and instantly shareable.

March 2025 — First Remixes

Lore Expansion

TikTok users begin creating their own edits. Some animate the character swinging his bat and exploding. Others draw him with mechanical pencils. The lore grows: Tung Tung Sahur is a punishment for ignoring sahur calls.

Late March 2025 — Ramadan Farewell

The Seasonal Goodbye

As Ramadan ends, Indonesian users create memes imagining Tung Tung Sahur returning home after fulfilling his duty. Videos show him walking away, boarding imaginary flights, or dramatically vanishing.

May 2025 — Roblox Explosion

Steal a Brainrot Launch

The Roblox game Steal a Brainrot launches with Tung Tung Sahur as a Rare-tier collectible. The game reaches over 20 million concurrent players. The character becomes a commercial asset.

September 2025 — Legal Dispute

Removal from Roblox

Noxa's licensing agency, Mementum Lab, files a cease-and-desist. Tung Tung Sahur is removed from Steal a Brainrot. The removal triggers a federal lawsuit about AI-generated character copyright.

January 2026 — AI-Free Art Trend

The Meta-Meme

An artist posts a hand-drawn version of Tung Tung Sahur with the caption "Found out that tung tung sahur is ai generated so I made an ai free version you can use instead." The post goes viral.

March 2026 — Triple T Dance

Dance Trend

A CapCut template featuring Tung Tung Sahur dancing to "Dumb wit it (get in dea)" receives over 1.8 million views. The dance becomes the character's most recognizable modern format.

April 2026 — Fortnite

Mainstream Gaming

Epic Games adds Tung Tung Sahur and Ballerina Cappuccina as cosmetic skins in Fortnite. The character reaches a mainstream gaming audience. The bundle includes a baseball bat pickaxe.

Four steps. No animation experience required. All you need is the character image, a template, and the right sound.

Get the Image

Use the original Tung Tung Sahur PNG or a green screen template. Crop the character cleanly.

2

Add the Sound

Use a Template
