🛠️ What You Need Before You Start

Three things. Nothing else. The character image, the audio, and the editor. Everything else is optional.

Character Image (PNG)

You need a clean image of Tung Tung Sahur with a transparent background. Search "Tung Tung Sahur PNG" or download the original AI-generated image and remove the background with a free tool. The image should show the wooden body, wide eyes, and baseball bat.

Signature Audio File

Find the original "tung tung tung sahur" sound on TikTok. Tap the audio name and use the "Save sound" option. The file saves to your device and can be imported into any editor. Do not replace the audio with music — the sound is the meme.

CapCut (or Similar Editor)

CapCut is free, available on iOS and Android, and has the largest library of Tung Tung Sahur templates. Alternatives include InShot, VN, and Adobe Premiere Rush. CapCut is recommended because its green screen templates are pre-matched to the character.

Stable Internet

You do not need a fast connection, but you need a stable one. CapCut's template library loads on demand, and TikTok's sound search requires an active connection. A slow connection will not stop you — a dropped connection mid-export will.

🎬 The Four Steps

Follow these in order. Skipping ahead is the most common reason new memes fail to gain traction.

1

Get the Character Image

Open CapCut and create a new project. Import the Tung Tung Sahur PNG. Use the auto-cutout tool to remove any remaining background. Place the character on the timeline as a sticker layer.

2

Add the Signature Audio

Import the saved audio file. Drag it onto the timeline and match the start to your character's first movement. Trim the audio so it ends with the punchline. Do not layer music over it — the original sound must be clear.

3

Use a Green Screen Template

Open the "Templates" tab and search "tung tung sahur". Choose one that matches your chosen format. Insert your character image into the placeholder. Adjust scale and position so the character looks natural in the scene.

4

Keep It Short and Punchy

Cut the final video to 8 to 15 seconds. The joke must land in the first 3 seconds. If your meme requires more than 15 seconds to make sense, the joke is not clear enough. Start over with a simpler idea.

⚙️ Technical Settings That Matter

Four settings in CapCut that determine whether your video reaches the "For You" page or dies at 200 views.

Aspect Ratio: 9:16

Always export in 9:16. TikTok, Reels, and Shorts all use vertical full-screen format. A 16:9 export looks small and gets fewer views. CapCut sets this automatically when you start a new TikTok project.

Frame Rate: 30 or 60 FPS

30 FPS is the minimum. 60 FPS looks smoother for dance edits and fast cuts. Avoid 24 FPS — it looks cinematic, which is the opposite of what a brainrot meme should look like. Export at 1080p minimum.

Remove the Watermark

The free version of CapCut adds a small logo at the end. To remove it, tap "Export" and uncheck "Add CapCut watermark". This option is available on both iOS and Android without a paid subscription.

Avoid Heavy Text Overlays

Do not cover the character with text. TikTok's algorithm reads text on screen but downweights videos with heavy overlays. If you need text, keep it to one short line at the bottom and remove it before the punchline.

🚫 Four Mistakes That Kill a Meme

Each of these mistakes is easy to make and easy to avoid. If your meme is not gaining views, it is almost certainly one of these four.

Replacing the Original Audio

If you add a music track over the original "tung tung tung sahur" sound, the meme loses its identity. The audio is not optional. It is the entire reason the meme works. Never replace it with a trending song.

Making It Too Long

Brainrot memes work because they are short and absurd. If your edit runs longer than 15 seconds, viewers scroll past before the punchline. Cut everything that is not the joke.

Using Low-Resolution Images

A blurry character image makes the entire video look low-quality. Download the highest-resolution version of the character you can find. If your image is under 800 pixels wide, it will look bad on a phone screen.

Forgetting the Caption

An empty caption is a missed opportunity. Write one short line that adds context or a joke. Use three to five hashtags — #tungtungsahur, #brainrot, #italianbrainrot, #fyp, #viral. Do not stuff hashtags.

📤 Publishing and Promoting

Posting is the final step. But when you post matters almost as much as what you post. Here is the timing that works.

Best posting times: For maximum reach on TikTok, post between 6 and 9 PM local time on weekdays, or 10 AM to noon on weekends. The "For You" algorithm pushes new videos into a small test audience in the first 30 minutes. Posting when your target audience is active gives you the best shot at a viral push.

Captions that work: Keep captions to one short sentence. Examples that perform well: "he heard you didn't wake up for sahur", "POV: you ignored the third call", "when the drum hits at 3 AM". Do not over-explain the joke — the absurdity is the point.

When to repost: If a video receives fewer than 200 views in the first hour, delete it and repost the same video at a different time. TikTok's algorithm sometimes drops a video for no reason. A repost is not cheating — it is a standard practice on the platform. Wait at least 24 hours before reposting to avoid a shadowban.

Cross-posting: Post the same video to Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts. Both platforms have a brainrot audience and will reward the video with its own algorithm. Instagram Reels tend to perform better with a caption, YouTube Shorts perform better with a title that includes "Tung Tung Sahur".
