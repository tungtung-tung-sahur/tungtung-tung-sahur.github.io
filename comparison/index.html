Version Comparison

Five different Tung Tung Sahur games exist. They share a character, a meme, and a lawsuit — but almost nothing else. One is a stealth adventure. One is a wave shooter. One is a horror escape. This page tells you which one fits the way you actually want to play.

Most players assume every Tung Tung Sahur game plays like the browser version on our home page. That is wrong. The character has been adapted into at least five distinct genres by five different teams, and the differences between them are larger than the differences between, say, Granny and Five Nights at Freddy's.

This page breaks down every version by genre, platform, difficulty, and what kind of player it suits. If you are deciding where to start, use the three cards below first. If you already know your playstyle, jump to the full comparison table.

🎯 Which One Is Right for You?

Three player profiles, three recommendations. Find the one that describes you and start there.

🌱 Beginner

You Just Found the Meme

Start with Baker Treasure Hunt — the browser version on our home page. It has no download, no account, and the gentlest tutorial. You learn the patrol loop, the detector budget, and the bread economy in about ten minutes.

🎮 Experienced

You Want a Real Challenge

Start with Horror Night with Tung Tung Tung Sahur. It takes the same character and drops him into a full escape-room horror game with puzzles, a pistol, and five Strange Trophies that almost nobody has found. It is the hardest version by a wide margin.

⚔️ Aggressive

You Want to Fight Back

Start with Brainrot Tung Tung Sahur. This is a wave-based arena shooter. You do not hide. You shoot. The bat becomes a melee weapon, the character becomes a target, and the tone shifts from stealth to chaos. The complete opposite of the browser version.

🎮 Version Breakdown

A closer look at the four versions most players actually try. Each card covers what makes that version unique — the one thing it does better than every other Tung Tung Sahur game.

🥖

Baker Treasure Hunt

Browser · Free · 3D Stealth

The version on our home page. You play a baker searching for the Golden Statue with a 40-pulse metal detector, a bread oven, and three lives. It is the most faithful adaptation of the original meme: the wooden character walks a fixed patrol loop, cannot hear you, and only reacts to movement. Everything you learn here carries over to the other versions.

⏱️ 8–15 min per run ❤️ 3 lives 🗺️ 4 zones
How to play →
🌙

Horror Night with Tung Tung Tung Sahur

PC, Switch, Epic · Paid · Escape Horror

The most content-rich version. You are trapped in a mansion with the kentongan, a pistol, and a set of puzzles that gate your escape. It has 26 Steam achievements, five hidden Strange Trophies, and the longest completion time of any Tung Tung Sahur game. This is where the "hard" version of the character lives — he hunts you across corridors instead of patrolling a forest.

⏱️ 4–6 hours to finish 🏆 26 achievements 🔫 Pistol included
Full achievement list →
🧠

Brainrot Tung Tung Sahur

PC, PlayStation · Paid · Arena Shooter

The version for players who do not want to hide. You fight waves of brainrot creatures across eight levels, with the character's signature bat as a melee weapon and a growing arsenal of ranged options. The combat system is simple but satisfying, and the boss battles at every second level give the game a real sense of escalation. The platinum trophy is achievable in about two hours.

⏱️ 2 hours to platinum 🏆 25 trophies ⚔️ Full combat
See on PlayStation →
🎮

Official Noxa Puzzle Game

iOS, Android · Free · Puzzle Escape

The only officially licensed version. You play as Tung Tung Sahur himself, breaking out of a prison by pushing boxes and solving spatial puzzles. The tone is light, the puzzles are forgiving, and the mobile controls are precise. This is the version to try if you want to know what the character feels like when he is not the threat.

⏱️ 1–2 hours 🎯 Puzzle-only 📱 Mobile-first

⚖️ Browser Version vs Paid Versions

The most common decision point. Here is what you gain and what you lose when you move from the free browser version to a paid one.

✅ Why the Free Browser Version Wins

  • No download, no install, no account required
  • Runs on school laptops and old tablets
  • The purest stealth loop — no combat mechanics to distract
  • Fast 8-minute runs make it easy to practice
  • Teaches every core mechanic that carries over to paid versions
  • Runs in any modern browser on any operating system

⚠️ What Paid Versions Add

  • Longer story campaigns with narrative progression
  • Full achievement and trophy support
  • Combat systems in the arena version
  • Multiple enemy types beyond the single kentongan character
  • Higher production values — voice acting, cinematic cutscenes
  • Cloud saves and cross-platform progress in some versions

✅ The Verdict

If you want one answer, here it is.

Start Free. Then Go Paid.

Play the browser version first. Finish at least one run, learn the patrol loop, and bake three perfect loaves. If you still want more after that, buy Horror Night for the story or Brainrot Tung Tung Sahur for the combat. Do not buy either one before you have finished a browser run — you will not appreciate what makes the paid versions different until you have felt the free one's rhythm.
