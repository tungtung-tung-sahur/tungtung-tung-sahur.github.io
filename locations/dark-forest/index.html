🌲 What the Dark Forest Actually Is

The zone looks like a wilderness. In practice, it is a training ground. Every mechanic in the game is introduced here in its simplest form — slow patrol, clear sight lines, and enough hiding spots that you can make three mistakes and still survive. But the zone is also where most players quit. Not because it is hard, but because they treat it like an action game instead of a stealth game.

You spawn next to the truck. The truck is your base of operations. It has an oven, a loading bay, and a small stack of ingredients. Your first task is not to explore. It is to bake bread. Three ingredients spawn within 30 meters of the truck. Collect them, return to the oven, and bake a loaf. A rushed loaf sells for 5 coins. A perfect loaf sells for 12. The difference is four seconds of patience.

The wooden character — Tung Tung Sahur — walks a fixed loop through the forest. He does not deviate from this loop unless something catches his eye. He cannot hear you. He only reacts to movement. That means crouching behind a tree is the same as being invisible. It also means sprinting across an open clearing is the same as waving a flag. Most new players get caught in the first 90 seconds because they run from the truck to the first ingredient without checking where the patrol is.

The Dark Forest is designed to teach you two things: watch before you move, and save your detector pulses. If you learn both, the rest of the map is manageable. If you ignore either, you will restart more times than you finish.

🔄 The Patrol Loop Explained

The wooden character walks the same path in the same order every run. The loop takes roughly 40 seconds to complete. Once you know it, you can predict where he will be at any moment.

1️⃣

North Path

He starts at the north edge of the map and walks south along the main dirt path. This is the longest straight section of the loop. It takes about 12 seconds. During this time, the west side of the map is safe.

2️⃣

Central Clearing

He crosses the central clearing — the largest open area in the zone. He slows down slightly here. This is the most dangerous spot for you if you are also in the clearing. But it is also the best spot to watch him from cover.

3️⃣

East Tree Line

He follows the eastern tree line north. This section has the most hiding spots — thick trunks, fallen logs, and two wooden huts. If you need to move during this phase, stay on the west side.

4️⃣

Loop Reset

He returns to the north edge and starts again. There is a brief pause of about 3 seconds at the top of the loop. That pause is your window to cross the central clearing if you need to.

🛖 Hiding Spots: Where to Go and When

The Dark Forest has six confirmed hiding huts, plus several natural cover points. Hiding in a hut is always safe — the character cannot enter. But entering a hut costs time, and time is the resource you spend on everything else.

1

Truck Hut

Directly behind the truck. The closest hiding spot to your spawn. Use it if the patrol catches you while you are baking bread. It is small and does not contain loot.

2

West Cabin

On the western edge of the map, about 25 meters from the truck. Contains a metal detector battery recharge. Worth entering if you have burned through more than 15 pulses.

3

East Cabin

On the eastern tree line, near the patrol's third phase. Contains one of the three bread ingredients. High risk, high reward — you will likely need to hide here at least once per run.

4

North Watchtower

A tall wooden structure on the northern edge. Not a hiding spot — an observation point. Climb it to see the patrol's full loop before you start moving. Costs about 8 seconds to climb and descend.

🥖 Bread Baking: The First Thing You Should Do

Bread is the only currency in the game. A perfect loaf earns 12 coins. A rushed loaf earns 5. The difference compounds over a full run: three perfect bakes are worth 36 coins; three rushed bakes are worth 15. The lucky charm — a fourth life for your next run — costs 30 coins.

The oven has a visible timer. The window for a perfect loaf is roughly 4 seconds. If you pull the bread before the timer hits the green zone, you get a rushed loaf. If you pull it after the timer passes the green zone, you get a burnt loaf worth 2 coins. The green zone is marked clearly on the oven interface. Wait for it.

Three ingredients spawn near the truck in every run. One is always within 30 meters. The other two are further out — usually near the east cabin and near the north watchtower. You do not need to collect all three before baking. You can bake one loaf, sell it, and then collect the next ingredient. This spreads your exposure over time instead of forcing you to cross the entire map in one trip.

🔍 Metal Detector: When to Scan and When to Save

The detector has 40 pulses per run. Holding the scan button drains the battery in 15 seconds. Tapping it gives you 40 separate chances to find a relic. The Golden Statue only appears on the detector in the final minutes of a run — not here.

🔋

Pulse, Don't Hold

Tapping the scan button gives you one pulse per tap. Holding it drains the entire battery in 15 seconds. There is no reason to hold the button. Ever.

📍

What You Can Find Here

The Dark Forest contains three buried relics: a coin cache, a metal detector battery recharge, and one of the three bread ingredients. None of these are the Golden Statue. Save at least 10 pulses for the Cursed Mansion.

🚫 Three Mistakes That End Runs in the Dark Forest

These are the three behaviors that account for the highest number of early failures in this zone. Each one is fixable in under a minute of practice.

🏃

Sprinting in the Open

The character reacts to movement, not sound. A crouched player behind a tree is invisible. A sprinting player in a clearing is a target. If you can see the sky, you can probably be seen.

🔋

Emptying the Detector Early

Using all 40 pulses in the first five minutes leaves you with no way to locate relics in the Graveyard or the Golden Statue in the Mansion. The Dark Forest has almost no valuable buried items. Save your pulses.

🥖

Rushing the Oven

A rushed loaf is 5 coins instead of 12. Over a full run that is a difference of 20 to 30 coins — enough to miss the lucky charm that would have saved your next attempt.
