🪦 What the Graveyard Actually Is

The zone looks like a graveyard. In practice, it is a listening test. The fog hides almost everything beyond arm's reach, and the wooden character moves faster than he does in the Dark Forest. You cannot rely on sight here. You have to rely on sound, memory, and the metal detector.

The Dark Forest taught you to watch before you move. The Graveyard punishes anyone who forgot that lesson. The patrol loop here is roughly 25 seconds — 15 seconds shorter than the Forest. That means every window of safety closes faster. A gap that would have given you eight seconds to cross in the Forest gives you five here. If you are not already at the next cover by the time the patrol turns the corner, you will be spotted.

The fog is the other half of the challenge. Visibility drops to about 8 meters. In the Forest, you could see the patrol from across the map and plan three moves ahead. Here, you see him when he is already close. The counter is simple in theory and hard in practice: move less. Every step you take is a risk. Every second you spend in the open is a second the patrol might appear.

But the Graveyard is also where the game starts rewarding skill. The bone charm is buried here, and it changes how you play every zone after this one. If you find it, the Golden Statue becomes visible through walls in the Cursed Mansion. If you skip it, you will be digging blind in the final zone. That is the trade: low visibility now, better visibility later.

🌫️ The Fog: How It Changes Everything

The fog is not just a visual filter. It removes information. You cannot see the patrol loop from a distance. You cannot see hiding spots from the other side of the map. You cannot see where the treasure is buried. Every decision becomes local instead of strategic.

👁️

8-Meter Visibility

You can see clearly to about 8 meters. Beyond that, shapes blur. Beyond 15 meters, everything is grey. This means you must physically explore the map to know where anything is.

👂

Sound Becomes Your Eyes

The wooden character's footsteps echo in the fog before he becomes visible. Listen for two distinct footsteps — one heavy thud, followed by a scrape. That rhythm tells you which direction he is coming from.

🕯️

Lanterns Mark Safe Zones

Small oil lanterns are placed on the corners of certain graves. Standing within 3 meters of a lantern improves your visibility slightly and lets you read the metal detector interface without straining. They also mark the safest places to pause.

🧭

Compass Still Works

Your compass at the top of the screen shows the direction of the truck and the direction of the nearest relic. Use it to navigate when visibility drops. It does not show the patrol — only fixed landmarks.

🔄 The Patrol Loop Explained

The patrol loop in the Graveyard is shorter and sharper than the Dark Forest loop. Instead of four wide phases, it has five tight phases. This is why it feels faster — even though the character moves at the same speed, he turns more often.

1

North Gate

He enters through the north iron gate and walks south along the central path. This phase lasts about 5 seconds. You can hear the gate creak before he appears.

2

Central Tomb

He circles the largest tomb in the center of the graveyard. This phase lasts about 4 seconds. During this time, the east side is the safest place to be.

3

East Row

He follows the eastern row of graves north. This phase lasts about 5 seconds. It is the longest stretch without a turn — and the best time to move if you are on the west side.

4

West Row

He crosses to the western row and walks south. This phase lasts about 6 seconds. There are fewer hiding spots on the west side — plan ahead before you move.

5

Loop Reset

He returns to the north gate. There is a 2-second pause before he re-enters. That pause is shorter than in the Dark Forest — do not count on it for a long move.

🦴 The Bone Charm: Location and How to Find It

The bone charm is buried under one of the graves. It is not marked on any map. You find it with the metal detector. Once you have it, the Golden Statue's location becomes visible through walls in every zone you have not yet completed.

The charm is buried in the northwest quadrant of the graveyard, under one of three graves. Which of the three varies by run — but the quadrant does not. If you sweep the northwest quadrant with short pulses, you will find it within 5 to 10 pulses.

The charm emits a distinct signal on the metal detector — a slow, regular pulse rather than the sharp spike of coins or tools. When you hear that slow pulse, dig immediately. The patrol loop will not pause for you. The charm is worth the risk: without it, the Cursed Mansion becomes a blind dig in a corridor with the patrol closing in from both ends.

"The bone charm does not show you what is buried. It shows you what was buried — outlines through walls. Every relic in every zone becomes a faint glowing shape."

In-game description of the bone charm

If you fail to find the charm on your first pass, do not spend more than 12 pulses on it. Save the rest for the Mansion. The charm is valuable, but it is not the win condition. The Golden Statue is.

🚫 Three Mistakes That End Runs in the Graveyard

These are the three behaviors that account for the highest number of failures in this zone. All of them are learnable in two or three runs.

🏃

Running Into the Fog

The fog hides the patrol from you — but it does not hide you from him. He sees movement clearly at 15 meters, even if you cannot see him. If you run into the fog, you are running into his line of sight without knowing it.

🔋

Wasting Pulses on Empty Ground

The Graveyard has three relics total: the bone charm, a coin cache, and a tool upgrade. If you sweep the whole map with the detector, you will burn 30+ pulses for three finds. Sweep the northwest quadrant first.

🕯️

Staying Near Lanterns Too Long

Lanterns improve visibility — but they also glow. The wooden character does not react to light. But he does see movement well, and if you are standing next to a lantern, you are the most visible thing in the fog. Move away from lanterns before the patrol turns your way.

The Graveyard is the last zone where you have room to recover from a mistake. The next two zones do not. The Abandoned Village will pull the patrol toward you on a 30-second timer, and the Cursed Mansion will lock you in corridors.
