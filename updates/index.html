Updates & Patch Notes

Every update for every Tung Tung Sahur game. From small bug fixes to major content drops, this is the complete changelog for all versions. Sources: Steam, Google Play, App Store, and the official game wikis.

📅 Update Timeline

A chronological view of every major update across all Tung Tung Sahur games. Newest first.

August 17, 2026

Tung Tung Tung Survival — Combat Balance Patch

Fixed Tung Tung Sahur not being able to deal damage outside his attack range. Reduced attack cancel range to 7 meters. Decreased stamina regeneration while moving. Increased idle stamina regeneration and crouched regeneration.

June 10, 2026

Ardent Wilds — Version 0.5.9

Added pre-generated worlds for faster loading. Reworked digging and mining. Added Poison Dagger Throw ability. Added minecart SFX. Updated main menu with new logo. Added area-based audio reverb.

May 12, 2026

Tung Tung Sahur: The Game — Version 3.0

Performance improvements and bug fixes. Fixed Tung Tung Sahur's animation when respawned. Fixed the issue where items could not be sold to Brr Brr Patapim. New brainrot characters teased for future updates.

April 15, 2026

Steal a Brainrot — Update 25

Tung Tung Tung Sahur returned as a Secret-tier character ($1.5M/s) after two months of copyright removal. Admin Machine added with random mutation, luck multiplier, and Brainrot/Lucky Block spawns.

April 1, 2026

Fortnite — Version 40.10

Tung Tung Sahur and Ballerina Cappuccina added as visual items (skins) in the Fortnite item shop. The bundle includes a baseball bat pickaxe.

March 21, 2026

Tung Tung Tung Sahur — Version 1.0.5

Google Play release. Minor bug fixes and stability improvements. Released globally on May 3, 2025.

December 5, 2025

Steal a Brainrot — Tung Tung Sahur Return Event

Tung Tung Tung Sahur officially returned after licensing issues were resolved. Admin Machine went live. Advent Calendar system introduced with daily login rewards. 12X Luck Multiplier for the event window.

November 29, 2025

Steal a Brainrot — Admin Abuse Session

Tung Tung Sahur briefly returned during an Admin Abuse session. Rather than simply restoring the old version, Sammy introduced a completely new interpretation of the character.

October 25, 2025

Tung Tung Tung Sahur Shooter — Version 0.11

Added epic boss battles at key score milestones. New wave-based system where enemies evolve as your score rises. Improved UI and gameplay balance. Fixed bugs and performance issues.

September 12, 2025

Horror Night with Tung Tung Tung Sahur — Nintendo Switch Release

Released on Nintendo Switch. 1.4 GB file size. Single-player action-adventure puzzle game. ESRB Teen rating.

August 2, 2025

Horror Night with Tung Tung Tung Sahur — Steam Achievements Fix

All achievements should now unlock correctly. Progress tracking has been verified and improved. Update notes via Steam Community.

May 30, 2025

Tung Tung Tung Sahur 3D Horror — Major Content Update

Added 15 new anomalies including Walid, Ireng Reng, Greng Greng, Budi Din din dung, BlueBerri Octopusini, Bales Chat Woi, Bobrini, Chimpazini Cocosini, Fafifufafa, Frigo Camelo, Glorbo, Kas kis kus kis kas, Tung Tung Hitam, Anpalilbabel, and Avocadorilla. Permanent Anomaly Unlock System using Tung Tung Tung Coins. Fixed LAG issues. Added Online Leaderboard System (Speed Challenge). Added Loading Menu.

May 7, 2025

Tung Tung Sahur: The Game — Launch

Released on App Store and Google Play. The baker protagonist, metal detector, Ramadan bread baking, and Golden Tung Tung Statue mechanics introduced. 196.5 MB download size. iOS 15.0+ required.

May 4, 2025

Tung Tung Sahur: The Game — Update 5 (v1.4)

Big performance boost — game runs much faster and smoother. Game size reduced. Traps added to the oven area. Bug with the mixer disappearing is fixed. All doughs in the oven now have countdown timers. New toy added: Tralalero Tralala.

April 30, 2025

Tung Tung Sahur: The Game — Update 4

Skibidi Car added — drive freely around the map. Brr Brr Patapim added — sell your items to him for rewards. Difficulty levels added. Golden Revolver, Unlimited Running, and 500 Money starter packs added. New items available in the store. Visual improvements.

April 2, 2025

Tung Tung Nightmare 3D — Release

Initial release on Google Play. 500,000+ downloads. The game features Tung Tung Tung Sahur as an anomalous creature, with Alomali Garam Ram Garam and Med Dung Tak Dung Manure as companion characters.

🚨 Major Milestones

Five events that changed the game permanently. Each one marked a turning point in the history of Tung Tung Sahur games.

📅

May 7, 2025 — Official Game Launch

The Noxa-approved Tung Tung Sahur: The Game launched on iOS and Android. This was the first officially licensed mobile game based on the character. It introduced the baker protagonist, metal detector, and Golden Statue mechanics that are now central to the series.

⚖️

September 2025 — Steal a Brainrot Removal

Tung Tung Sahur was removed from Steal a Brainrot after Mementum Lab filed a cease-and-desist. The character had been one of the most valuable collectibles in the game. The removal triggered a federal lawsuit that remains unresolved.

🔄

November 29, 2025 — Character Return

During an Admin Abuse session, Tung Tung Sahur returned to Steal a Brainrot. Rather than simply restoring the old version, the developer introduced a completely new interpretation of the character. This was the first sign that the licensing dispute was easing.

🎮

April 1, 2026 — Fortnite Integration

Tung Tung Sahur and Ballerina Cappuccina were added as visual items in Fortnite's item shop. This brought the character to a mainstream gaming audience. The bundle included a baseball bat pickaxe.

🏆

May 12, 2026 — Version 3.0

The largest update to Tung Tung Sahur: The Game. Brought performance improvements, fixed several long-standing bugs, and teased new brainrot characters for future updates. This update marked one year since the game's original release.

🔮

Future Plans

Several updates have been confirmed but not yet released. Tung Tung Sahur: The Game will add Brr Brr Patapim, Tralalero Tralala, and Crocodilo Bombardino as playable characters. Steal a Brainrot may introduce new seasonal events. No official release dates have been announced.
