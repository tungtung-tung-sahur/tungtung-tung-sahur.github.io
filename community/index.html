Original Creator

Noxa — The Origin

The Indonesian TikTok creator who posted the first clip that started everything. Noxa's original DALL-E generation, the nine-tung voiceover, and the character design remain the foundation the entire community is built on. Every edit, every remix, and every game traces back to a single 24-second video.

Community Calendar

Every Friday · 9 PM GMT

Speedrun Race Night

Runners from the community race the Any% category live. Spectators vote on the fastest route and the most creative shortcuts. Results are posted the following Saturday.

First Saturday of the Month

Fan Art Challenge

A themed prompt is announced. Artists have one week to submit. Community votes pick the winner. Winning art is featured on the community sidebar for the next month.

Every Sunday · 4 PM EST

Discord Voice Hangout

Open voice channel where fans talk about the meme, share clips, and coordinate collabs. No agenda. Drop in, listen, leave whenever.

Ramadan Start · Yearly

The Original Rewatch

On the anniversary of the first TikTok upload, the community collectively rewatches the original clip and shares what the meme meant to them that year.

When a New Game Launches

Launch Day Watch Party

Fans gather in Discord and on Twitch to play the newest Tung Tung Sahur game together on day one. First reactions, bug reports, and route discoveries are shared live.

Last Weekend of Each Season

Community Retrospective

A recap thread of the best memes, videos, and art from the past three months. Members vote for their favorite fan creation of the season.

How to Join

Start on TikTok

Search "tung tung sahur" and scroll the latest videos. Save the sound. Follow three creators whose style you like. This is your entry point.

Find a Discord

Servers are shared in TikTok bios and Reddit sidebars. Read the rules, introduce yourself, and pick one channel to start. Do not post in every channel at once.

Make Something

The community rewards creators. One video, one drawing, one remix. Post it and credit your sources. That is how everyone else started.
