🎮 Games with Achievements

Five different Tung Tung Sahur games track achievements or trophies. Each one has a different set, a different platform, and a different completion time.

🌙

Horror Night with Tung Tung Tung Sahur

26 Achievements · Steam / Epic

A first-person horror escape game. You are trapped in a mansion with the kentongan and several other brainrot characters. Solve puzzles, avoid detection, and escape. The achievements cover puzzle completion, story progression, and hidden collectibles.

See on Steam →
🧠

Brainrot Tung Tung Sahur

25 Trophies · PlayStation

A wave-based action shooter. You fight off waves of brainrot creatures across five maps. The trophies track level completion, weapon unlocks, and specific challenge kills. Platinum is obtainable in about 2 hours.

See on PlayStation →
🏃

Tung Tung Tung Sahur Runner

12 Trophies · PlayStation

An endless runner where you collect "Sahurs" by surviving as long as possible. The trophies are straightforward: collect 1 through 8 Sahurs in a single run. 99% of players earn the platinum.

See on PlayStation →
🪂

Tung Tung Sahur Extreme

23 Trophies · PlayStation

A parachute-based arcade game. You jump from planes, fly through rings, and collect coins. The trophies track altitude, coin totals, ring counts, and parachute customization.

💡 Tips for Hard Achievements

Three achievements in Horror Night have a 0% or near-0% unlock rate. Here is how to get them.

🏆

Trophy of the Forgotten II

The second Strange Trophy is in the basement, behind a crate that requires the ladder puzzle to reach. Place the ladder, climb down, and look behind the third crate on the left. Rarity: 0% — almost no one has found it.

🏆

Trophy of the Forgotten V

The fifth Strange Trophy is on the roof. You must escape through the window (Desperate Leap achievement) and then climb to the highest point of the mansion. The trophy is on the chimney. Rarity: 0%.

📰

Tabloid Terrorist

Six photos of Tralalero Tralala are hidden across the mansion. You need the Night Vision camera upgrade first. The photos are in: the study, the ballroom, the cellar, the greenhouse, the attic, and the kitchen pantry. Rarity: 0%.

🎯

No More Nightmares (Platinum)

Requires all 26 achievements. The hardest part is the five Strange Trophies. Get those first, then clean up the puzzle and story achievements. Full completion time: 4 to 6 hours.
